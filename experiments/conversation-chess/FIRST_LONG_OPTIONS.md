## Selected: Tony versus Richie, from the jacket to open defiance

Tony is central throughout, the adversary stays identifiable, and Richie's
attempts to assert status provide both tactical analysis and comic reactions.
The jacket is a recognizable hook, followed by a real escalation rather than
a disconnected compilation of quotes. This angle is selected; its published
title and thumbnail are deferred. It is not a prediction of views.

| Chapter | Episode, verified | What the exchange adds |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge the new hierarchy | S2E3, *Toodle-Fucking-Oo* | Richie returns; Tony tries to limit his treatment of Beansie. Old status clashes with present authority. |
| The jacket | S2E8, *Full Leather Jacket* | Richie presents the Rocco DiMeo trophy; Tony responds politely; the gift does not restore the status Richie expects. |
| An instruction is ignored | S2E11, *House Arrest* | Selling drugs on garbage routes threatens the legitimate business. Tony orders Richie to stop; Richie resists. |
| Respect is not inherited | S2E12, *Knight in White Satin Armor* | Tony sanctions Richie in the route meeting; Jackie Jr invokes his father's authority. Tony answers, “Those who want respect, give respect.” |

That last line is in **S2E12**, not the S2E3 introduction. Public unofficial
transcripts confirm the words and episode, but not exact speaker timing or
shot selection. Those must be verified on the actual HD source.

Proposed annotation logic: an attempted status gift can be Book/Good depending
on the chosen convention; continuing to break an explicit instruction can be
a Mistake or Blunder when the consequence is shown; Tony's reply cuts off the
appeal to inherited rank. These grades are editorial proposals, not reviewed
ratings. A threat is not automatically Brilliant simply because Tony says it.
Keep the jokes on the characters' failed tactics, such as expecting an old
jacket to purchase current authority. Do not annotate every sentence.

**Duration budget:** seek 8–10 minutes of distinct, intact relevant dialogue,
then about 25–35 substantial analysis pauses, intro and the revised results
screen. This could support approximately 11–15 minutes. The two public rivalry
compilations below total 14 minutes, but may overlap and include unrelated
material: their sum is not our usable footage duration. Stop the dialogue arc
before the domestic murder. Do not claim Tony personally kills Richie or
convert Janice's independent action into a Tony move.

## Alternative 2: Tony versus Feech — the old-school challenge

**Working title: Feech Thinks Tony Is Still a Kid | Sopranos Chess Analysis.**
Use the landscaping conflict in **S5E3, Where's Johnny?**, then the card-game
stories and stolen-car confrontation in **S5E4, All Happy Families...**. The
contrast is clear: Feech speaks as a senior legend, while Tony needs him to
accept the current chain of command. The “in your day” reprimand is in
**S5E4**, as confirmed in its public transcript. The poker story about young
Tony robbing Feech's game is also retold in S5E4; do not label that flashback
story as a newly depicted robbery or put this exchange in S5E2.

Comic beats include interminable anecdotes, hollow courtesy, a reaction to
Tony's joke and Feech treating Tony as a child. A final consequence can be
shown briefly, but his parole arrest is a separate scene, not continuous
dialogue. Name the chapter/transition and retain a single Tony/Feech bar
meaning: control of the disagreement. Tony's off-screen plan needs evidence
from the episode; do not invent mind-reading or rate every supporting player.

## Alternative 3: Tony versus Ralph — no order needed

**Working title: Tony Gives Ralph No Good Move | Sopranos Chess Analysis.**
The Jackie Jr decision in **S3E12, Amour Fou**, followed by the short pressure
conversation in **S3E13, Army of One**, is the strongest pure verbal trap in
this shortlist. Tony puts the choice onto Ralph and then invokes captaincy,
timeliness and chain of command. Our interpretation should explain the
available choices, not claim that every implied intention is an objective fact.

[The principal S3E12 exchange](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=neVu3AQAJNo)
has 3:25 / 1,630,056 catalogue views in the earlier same-day research.
[The S3E13 follow-up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U3y4ZLoRje0)
is 1:15 / 209 in the new search. The pair supports a strong shorter analysis,
roughly 7–10 minutes with useful pauses. It does **not** establish enough
distinct dialogue for a 15-minute first episode. Adding unrelated Ralph
conflicts solely to hit duration would weaken the precise premise. The prior
request to obtain the first clip failed at the provider; no Tony/Ralph HD media
is established here.

## Shorter future option: Tony versus Junior — the varsity athlete insult

**Working title: Junior Finds Tony's Weakest Square | Sopranos Chess Analysis.**
The recurring insult and family dinner in **S5E3, Where's Johnny?** are highly
recognizable and immediately comic. Tony's attempt to demand respect makes
the repetition more effective. The main dinner upload,
[The Soprano Sunday Dinner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sGK_uL3OeBU),
is 2:40 / 2,144,589 catalogue views; another
[5.03 upload](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-fjztq3SwW4) is 2:51 / 325,735.
These are largely the same scene, not five minutes of distinct material.

Best as a short standalone episode or a future Short. A 15-minute version
would require a different broad Tony/Junior arc, not just this insult. Junior's
cognitive decline and the later “don't you love me?” scene should not become
comic evidence of calculated tactical genius. A season-one boss negotiation
could support a separate long episode, but mixing it into this joke would
change the premise and require a new continuity plan.

## Rules before constructing the selected long episode

1. **White opens every video.** Review the opening in the actual source and
   assign White to the player who initiates the included exchange. If Richie
   starts it, Richie is White; if Tony starts it, Tony is White. Preserve those
   assignments throughout the video. Do not swap colors between chapters or
   assume Tony is always Black. Do not fabricate a lead-in or reorder words to
   make a convenient opening. The scene file must record the reviewed opener.
2. Only the user-approved Chess.com categories; **Miss and the reference's
   purple Interesting category are excluded by the latest user instruction**.
   Both pawns use the same silhouette in opposite colors. Results count actual
   included annotations once; commentary replays are not additional moves.
3. Preserve the validated music/SFX/typing rhythm and approved Tony pawn.
   Native Kdenlive project and export; English originals and written analysis,
   no voice-over. Analysis text must add a point rather than prolong a pause.
4. Acquire genuinely detailed 1080p-or-better source material ourselves, then inspect
   images, codec/bitrate and dialogue. A 1080p export from a soft 720p source is
   not a source upgrade. No new source of that quality is established here.
5. Source-relative timing, speakers, episode boundaries and reactions must be
   reviewed again on the chosen files. A transcript has no reliable timecodes.
   Leave references as links; series footage and raw API responses stay out of
   the public Git repository. Public availability is not a reuse licence.
