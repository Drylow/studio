## Reference match

The reference creator credits Sneaky Snitch for the intro and Scheming Weasel (faster version) for the outro. A normalized PCM waveform comparison of the reference's first 12 seconds against the creator's original Sneaky Snitch recording finds a match at **66.103875 seconds**, correlation **0.96646**. Use that section for the recognisable intro, then fade before dialogue. This is measured waveform evidence, not a claim of human listening. Reference clips remain in private temporary research storage and are not redistributed here.
