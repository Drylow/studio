## What the analysis argues

LaPadite invokes an official inspection and supplies a qualified escape rumor;
Landa counters with administrative procedure, a reputation test, coercive
rhetoric and a manufactured choice. The English-language pretext is explained
by the actual final dialogue. The scene is not a lesson in accepting his racist
analogy: the written analysis explicitly identifies its invalid inference.

At the restaurant, Shosanna maintains her present identity. He controls the
subject, her opportunity to finish an answer and the timing of the dessert.
The final question is never specified, so denying it would offer a subject he
has not named. The footage proves neither conscious recognition nor safety.
Her fear and LaPadite’s coerced admissions are not called blunders.

The categories judge conversational choices, not moral merit or engine-calculated
odds. Great and Best remain neutral; Book changes the display modestly.

reveal of the language trap, rather than guessing at a supernatural insight.
