Eleven conversations. One detective who never believed the smile.

From a backhanded compliment in the lab to a cage in the Everglades, Sergeant James Doakes and Dexter Morgan turn small talk, surveillance and a cabin standoff into a battle for control. We follow eleven exchanges from Dexter Seasons 1–2 and analyze their choices with chess move ratings and written commentary.

Original English dialogue, English captions and 32 written analyses. No voice-over.

Spoilers for Dexter Seasons 1–2.
