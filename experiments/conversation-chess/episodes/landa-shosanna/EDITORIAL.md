## What the episode examines

The encounter turns courtesy, dessert and ordinary questions into pressure.
Landa controls the agenda and pace; Shosanna's immediate objective is to keep
her present identity intact and get through the encounter. Her distress is not
a conversational blunder. The analysis must distinguish visible behavior from
what either character might secretly know.

Do not assert that Landa recognizes Shosanna. The film's ambiguity is part of
the scene, not a missing fact to invent. Ordering milk may create an association
for the viewer; it does not establish what Landa knows. Do not call intimidation
morally admirable: chess categories describe the exchange, not the character.
