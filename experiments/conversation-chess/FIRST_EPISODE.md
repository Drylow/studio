## Editorial direction

**No voice-over, synthetic narration or spoken explanation.** Keep the original dialogue and reactions. The Tony pawn comments through short original text and restrained visual reactions; no simulated talking mouth. Preserve room for the actors, readable subtitles and the evaluation bar.

The bar always means **control of this conversation: Tony / Ralph**. It is a humorous editorial reading, not an engine calculation, moral rating or prediction of Jackie’s survival. Giving someone mercy is not a “bad move” morally. The analysis concerns how responsibility and leverage are passed between the two speakers.

Our reading: Ralph tries to move the final decision to Tony. Tony returns the decision, coupled with reminders of Ralph’s position and personal obligations. The result is ambiguous enough to discuss; do not cut the scene to fabricate an explicit order Tony never gives.
