## One rivalry, four chapters

Keep the scenes in chronological order. The jacket is the main set piece and
the organizing motif: Richie tries to cash in his old reputation, but Tony
controls the rules now. Each subsequent conflict tests the same expectation.
Use short, explicit chapter cards for episode changes; do not present scenes
weeks apart as one uninterrupted conversation. The guide can include the
plain premise **“Richie wants respect. Tony wants obedience.”**

The evaluation bar tracks the two characters' control of this relationship in
the included exchanges. It does not rate morality, danger to Beansie or the
outcome of a real chess game. Start at 0.0, and maintain **one editorial game**
across the chapters: fixed colors, inherited last score, no secret reset to
equality. A scene change alone does not move the bar. Any change must follow a
reviewed move or a clearly shown consequence. Great/Best do not automatically
improve a position; do not inflate the score merely because Tony has a good line.

## Analysis opportunities, before timing review

The examples below are **original draft commentary**, not character dialogue
or final reviewed grades. Give each card a genuine tactical reason and, where
useful, the alternative that would have avoided the mistake. Do not grade an
injury, illness or addiction as a clever move.

| Visible decision to examine | Draft commentary idea | Editorial caution |
|---|---|---|
| Tony names a boundary around Beansie | “The new boss makes the rule explicit. Richie can comply, or reveal that he rejects the hierarchy.” | Verify the included opening and first speaker first. |
| Richie deflects the instruction | “A comeback is not compliance. The threat still exists after the punchline.” | If using the driving joke, the humour belongs to the character's deflection; do not celebrate Beansie's injury. |
| Richie presents the trophy | “He offers a gift, but keeps telling us who he beat to get it.” | Ambiguous motive: gesture of reconciliation, status claim or both. Present an interpretation, not a proven secret plan. |

| Richie asks why Tony does not wear it | “He is checking whether the gift produced the respect he expected.” | Preserve Tony's answer before judging the exchange. |
