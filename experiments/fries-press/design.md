# Fries / hydraulic press — approved series style

Use the Watermelon and Chocolate visual language: 216×384 painted pixels enlarged exactly five times to 1080×1920, nearest-neighbour, restrained palette, warm cream world, oak cutting board, green mechanical frame, clean white chef sleeve, chunky low-poly 3D. Reuse the hand geometry and bitmap lettering, not photographs or emoji.

## Palette

Background #f1e7d6; ink #203c2a; frame #244c37, #47714a, #6e9852, #a5bb76; pink accents #ef5867, #ff8291, #b93751, #f9af9f; cream #f2f0c4; oak #c79565, #dfb27e, #eacd9b; olive neutral #b4ac91, #6b6c51; steel #b7c2ba, #def2e1; dark #352922, #16201f; skin #efb07a, #ffd09b; fry / oil highlight #ffe59a; toasted fry #704530, #a46b46, #563426, #885438. Quantize all lighting to this palette. Golden oil uses #dfb27e, #efb07a and #ffe59a, with pale specular streaks.

## Type and composition

Food-specific amber extension: #d88f28, #f4ba45 and #fff0a6. Oil uses these saturated golds so it reads as wet runoff separately from the pale fries and oak board.

Existing hand-drawn 5×7 bitmap alphabet, on integer coordinates. Food icon beside FRIES; changing fry count in the top field; small HYDRAULIC PRESS label underneath. Protected title y=0–92; the press and food occupy y=93–327. Small three-load strip at y=340–375, replacing round captions. The action has priority over decorations; industrial frame legs stay behind the food. A close view can crop the very top of the fixed press frame but must show ram, plate, contact and escaping oil.

## Motion / evidence plan

- Each load: a supported tray enters, hand withdraws, ram approaches, fries buckle under contact, plate squeezes, oil escapes, then plate lifts. Camera uses the multi-phase-camera rule, adapted to a real perspective camera: wide carry, tighter contact, low front runoff, wider reset.
- Fry fragments and oil drops use the particle-burst rule: seeded launch values and analytic trajectories. Fragments retain irregular lengths and toasted surfaces; no neat cubes or floating debris after settling.
- Pressure indicator uses stat-bars-and-fills: continuous compression state, filled from the left. It indicates cartoon progress, not a real pressure measurement.
- Closing repeats the approved happy sleeve/spatula hop and small confetti bursts anchored to the projected hand.

## Constraints

No generic web fonts, smooth vector UI, blur, photoreal inserts, French text, destructive intro preview, gore, real brands, simulated physical pressure claims, silent impacts, or oil before squeezing. Don't extend the film with a frozen hold: the runoff camera and drops remain active during the payoff.
