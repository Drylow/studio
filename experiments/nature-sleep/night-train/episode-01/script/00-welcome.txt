Welcome to Quiet Little Worlds. Tonight, there is a place waiting for you beside a rain-streaked window. An evening train is making its gentle way through the hills, and you have all the time you need.

The lamp above your seat casts a warm circle on the wood. A small cup of tea rests on the table. Beyond the glass, the fir trees pass slowly through a veil of mist. You can settle into the soft cushions and let your shoulders find a comfortable place. Nothing needs to be finished tonight.

On the other end of the seat, a ginger cat named Pip has curled himself into the blanket. He lifts his head just long enough to notice that you are comfortable, then closes his eyes again. The carriage is warm. The rain stays on the other side of the glass.

Mara, the steward, brings a small wooden box to your table. Inside is a collection of postcards from the quiet places along this imaginary railway. Each picture holds an ordinary afternoon, a welcoming room, and people taking gentle care of the things around them. You can listen for as long as you wish, or let the words become part of the rain. Mara places the first card beneath the light.
