Welcome to Silent Little Worlds. Tonight, there is a small garden waiting beneath the evening rain. A lantern glows under the wooden roof of a pergola, and a warm blanket lies across the bench. Beyond it, a greenhouse keeps its gentle amber light. The path is wet, but your little corner is dry. You can settle here for as long as you like.

Mara, who looks after the garden, has left a cup of tea on the table beside you. A ginger cat named Pip is already asleep on the blanket. He has found the warmest fold and sees no reason to move. Across the path, rain draws widening circles on the pond. The leaves catch a few drops, then let them fall.

There is nothing to solve in the stories tonight, and nothing you need to remember. We will wander only in our imagination, through the greenhouse and the little rooms around this garden, meeting the people who tend them. You can follow their quiet work, or let the words become part of the rain. Whenever sleep arrives, there is no need to stay for the ending.

For now, the cup rests beside you, the lantern holds its warm light, and the first story begins with someone tending the lamps.
