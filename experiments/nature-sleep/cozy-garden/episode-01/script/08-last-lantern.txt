Pip stretches beneath the lamp. He reaches his front paws across the blanket, opens them into small soft fans, and lets his body lengthen behind them. Then he gathers himself again with the unhurried care of a cat arranging a favourite place. His tail finds the curve of his side. A little while later, his eyes are closed.

Mara comes to collect the empty dishes. She places them on a padded tray, so that the cups make only a small, rounded sound as they settle. The teapot she leaves with you.
