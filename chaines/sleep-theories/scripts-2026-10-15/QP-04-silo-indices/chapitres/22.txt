Walker's appeal to Carla shows how access to materials can affect an outcome even when neither person can cancel the official order. Ed's theft from Critical Supply gives that access a different use: removing goods that other residents depend on.

That puts Supply in a different position from the residents watching a cleaning. Most people encounter the suit as a finished object. By the time Juliette wears it, its materials have already passed through other hands. The people preparing that equipment have opportunities unavailable to someone standing in the cafeteria. A resident can hope Juliette survives, or argue about whether she deserves her punishment. Someone with access to the materials can help change the conditions under which she steps outside.

Critical Supply enters a different kind of story when Billings learns about Ed Harwood's thefts in the third season. Here, access allows someone to remove materials without authorization. The discovery gives Billings an explanation for how Ed has been taking them. Alongside the earlier tape intervention, it draws attention to a part of silo life that can disappear behind the larger mysteries. Stored goods have custodians, and the routes by which people reach those goods can affect what remains available for everyone else.

Carla's help and Ed's stealing need to stay attached to their separate circumstances. Walker approaches Carla because Juliette is about to be sent outside in equipment Walker has reason to question. Ed uses his unauthorized infrastructure to steal from Critical Supply. Their actions don't establish a shared purpose or a common organization. What connects the stories is the practical importance of access. In each case, the person who can reach materials can do something that a resident without that access can't accomplish merely by wanting a different outcome.

On a rewatch, that gives the departments a more tangible place in the story. Mechanical uses materials, IT holds its own stock, and Supply can help determine what reaches a person at a decisive moment. Critical Supply can also be stolen from by someone who gains unauthorized access. None of those facts makes the people handling goods into a single hidden faction. They give individual actions room to matter. Walker goes to Carla with a concern about tape, and Juliette eventually steps outside wearing a suit whose seals hold long enough for her to keep walking.
