TITLE
The Entire History of the Old Republic to Fall Asleep To | Star Wars Legends

DESCRIPTION
Settle in for a long journey through the Old Republic in Star Wars Legends, from the ancient communities of Tython to the wars and reforms that reshape the galaxy. Told at an unhurried pace, this history follows the worlds, institutions, and people caught between successive Jedi and Sith conflicts.

We travel through the Rakatan legacy, early Republic connections, the Great Sith War, the Mandalorian Wars, Revan and Malak, the Jedi Exile, and the return of the Sith Empire. The journey also visits the Treaty of Coruscant, the Eternal Empire, and the later struggle that leads toward Darth Bane and Ruusan. Player choices are distinguished from the broader history rather than treated as one inevitable outcome.

Spoilers: includes Dawn of the Jedi, Tales of the Jedi, both Knights of the Old Republic games, The Old Republic, and the Darth Bane era.
