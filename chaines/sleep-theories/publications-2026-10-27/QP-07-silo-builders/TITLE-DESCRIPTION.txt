TITLE
Who Built the Silos — and Why? | Silo Theories to Fall Asleep To

DESCRIPTION
Who built the silos, and how did a project meant to preserve humanity become the world its residents know? Settle in for a slow exploration of Silo's builders, hidden history, and competing explanations for the system they left behind.

We begin with everyday life underground, where people can maintain an extraordinary structure without knowing its origins. From the Pact, relics, and restricted knowledge, the journey leads into the Before Times and the people involved in creating the project. We examine how stated intentions, institutional decisions, and generations of secrecy change the meaning of survival. Confirmed events, character explanations, and theories are considered separately as we follow the evidence.

Spoilers: includes the television series through the season-three finale, Troy. No spoilers from the novels.

This is a long, unhurried companion for quiet evenings and the hours before sleep. Subscribe to Quiet Paradox for more mysteries, and leave a comment telling us where you're listening from and whether it's day or night there.
