TITLE
The Monsters, Talismans & Rules of FROM Explained to Fall Asleep To

DESCRIPTION
Explore the monsters, talismans, and uncertain rules of FROM in a long, calm narration for the hours before sleep. We follow the practical discoveries that help the town survive, along with the exceptions that make every rule harder to trust.

Starting with a visitor at a window, we examine protected spaces, invitations, familiar voices, the creatures' behavior, and Boyd's discovery of the stones. The journey continues through Smiley, the music box, the dangers beyond locked doors, and the changing threats faced by the community. Rather than assuming every explanation is settled, we distinguish what the characters observe from what they believe and what remains open to interpretation.

Spoilers: covers FROM through the season-four finale, If a Tree Falls in the Forest... This narration discusses the show's danger without graphic detail.

Settle in wherever this evening finds you. Subscribe to Quiet Paradox for more slow explorations, and tell us where you're listening from and whether there's daylight or darkness outside.
