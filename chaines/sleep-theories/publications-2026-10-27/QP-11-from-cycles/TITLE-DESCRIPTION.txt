TITLE
The Children, the Trees & the Cycles of FROM | Theories to Fall Asleep To

DESCRIPTION
Follow the children, the trees, and the repeating patterns of FROM in a slow exploration of memory, lost families, and attempts to change an old story. We begin beside the bottle tree, where a familiar melody connects Jade and Tabitha to something they could not reach alone.

The journey brings together Victor's drawings, Miranda's paintings, the recurring symbol, the children's message, and the uncertain routes offered by the trees. We also examine Julie's encounters with the past, the Boy in White, and the choices that reshape the town's latest crisis. Repetition gives the characters clues, but it does not make every outcome certain. Confirmed events and possible explanations are kept distinct throughout.

Spoilers: includes FROM through the season-four finale, If a Tree Falls in the Forest...
