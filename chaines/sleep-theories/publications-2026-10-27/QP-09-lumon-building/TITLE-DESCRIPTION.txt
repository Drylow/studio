What Is Lumon Really Building? | Severance Theories to Fall Asleep To

DESCRIPTION
What is Lumon really building beneath the ordinary routines of office work? Settle in for an unhurried journey through Severance, exploring the experiments, hidden rooms, and divided lives that bring us to Cold Harbor.

We follow the separation between innies and outies, the numbers of Macrodata Refinement, the Overtime Contingency, and the research surrounding Gemma. Mark's work, Cobel's discoveries, and the Eagan family's ambitions provide different ways of approaching the company's larger purpose. From emotional isolation to the possibility of expanding severance beyond the office, each theory is considered alongside what the series actually shows. Lumon's ultimate plans remain questions to examine, rather than answers we pretend are confirmed.

Spoilers: includes both seasons of Severance through the season-two finale, Cold Harbor.
