DESCRIPTION
Settle in for a quiet journey through the secrets of the Bene Gesserit, from Paul's test on Caladan to the uncertain future of Chapterhouse. This long Dune lore narration follows the Sisterhood's training, political influence, breeding program, and carefully prepared beliefs across generations.

We explore Jessica's choices, the Voice, Other Memory, the Missionaria Protectiva, and the consequences of a prophecy that escapes its planners. Along the way, Irulan, Alia, Leto II, Taraza, Odrade, Sheeana, and Murbella reveal how the Sisterhood adapts when its knowledge no longer guarantees control. The story returns to the people who must live with decisions made decades or centuries before them.

Spoilers: this video covers Frank Herbert's original six Dune novels, through Chapterhouse: Dune.
