The Rise and Fall of the Jedi Order Explained to Fall Asleep To | Star Wars Lore

DESCRIPTION
Fall asleep to the rise and fall of the Jedi Order, following its classrooms, councils, battles, and surviving teachers through the Star Wars galaxy. This calm, long narration explores how an institution devoted to service became entangled in the Republic's deepest crisis.

From the High Republic and life inside the Coruscant Temple, we follow the relationships between masters and apprentices, Dooku's disillusionment, the Clone Wars, Ahsoka's departure, and the destruction of Order 66. The journey continues with those who preserve fragments of Jedi teaching, including Obi-Wan, Yoda, Kanan, Luke, and Rey. At its heart is a question about what can survive when the institution carrying a tradition is lost.

Spoilers: this account follows the films and series in Star Wars canon, including the sequel trilogy.
