TITLE
The Great Houses of Dune Explained to Fall Asleep To | Dune Lore

DESCRIPTION
Explore the Great Houses of Dune in a calm, extended journey through the arrangements that hold the Imperium together. Behind each noble name are households, planets, debts, marriages, soldiers, and institutions that even the most powerful duke cannot simply command.

Beginning with the Atreides move from Caladan to Arrakis, we examine the Landsraad, CHOAM, the Spacing Guild, and the balance between the throne and the aristocracy. House Atreides, House Harkonnen, and House Corrino reveal different ways of exercising power. Mentats, physicians, swordmasters, heirs, and political marriages show how that power depends on individual loyalties. The story continues through Paul, Leto II, and the changing forms of authority after the Scattering.

Spoilers: this account covers Frank Herbert's original six Dune novels, through Chapterhouse: Dune.
