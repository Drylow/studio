The Entire History of the Fremen Explained to Fall Asleep To | Dune Lore

DESCRIPTION
Journey into the history of the Fremen, from the customs that make life possible on Arrakis to the changes brought by conquest, empire, and a greener world. This long, unhurried Dune lore narration follows a people whose relationship with water shapes almost every part of daily life.

We explore stillsuits, sietches, communal water, worm riding, leadership, and the ecological ambitions of Pardot and Liet Kynes. Then we follow the arrival of the Atreides, Paul and Jessica's place among the Fremen, Chani's life within the empire, and the consequences of transforming the desert. The later story asks what remains of a culture when its environment, political power, and living traditions have all changed.

Spoilers: this video covers Frank Herbert's original six Dune novels, through Chapterhouse: Dune.
