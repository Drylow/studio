## Description

They keep your wooden tag. Your mother waits outside with silver and a question nobody answers.
You enter the Qing palace with her green hairpin and a ledger. Your rank rises. Your room gets warmer. The letter you write home stays folded.
This is Beijing. This is palace life. This is what it costs to be remembered at court.
