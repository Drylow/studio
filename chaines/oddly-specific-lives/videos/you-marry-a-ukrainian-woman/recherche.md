# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Marry a Ukrainian Woman

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

VERIFIED FACTS about her world (use several, rounded, each inside a scene, with the source named in the sentence; never invent other statistics):
- Chicago's Ukrainian Village neighbourhood, with St. Nicholas Ukrainian Catholic Cathedral and the Ukrainian National Museum, has been a centre of Ukrainian life in America for over a century.
- In July 2022 UNESCO added the culture of Ukrainian borscht cooking to its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding.
- In a traditional Ukrainian wedding the couple stand on a rushnyk, an embroidered ritual towel; folk belief says whoever steps on it first will lead the family. The korovai, a richly decorated round wedding bread, is shared with the guests.
- Old Ukrainian proposal tradition: matchmakers (starosty) go with the groom to the bride's family; a bride who refuses gives the groom a pumpkin ("to give a pumpkin" still means to turn someone down).
- The vyshyvanka is the embroidered shirt; each region has its own patterns; Vyshyvanka Day is the third Thursday of May.
- Christmas Eve (Sviat Vechir) has twelve meatless dishes, starting with kutia; since 2023 most Ukrainian churches celebrate Christmas on December 25 instead of January 7.
- Pysanky are Easter eggs decorated with beeswax and dyes; the town of Kolomyia has a museum shaped like a giant pysanka.
- Common home superstitions: never shake hands across a doorway, don't whistle indoors ("the money will fly away"), and sit down for a moment in silence before a long journey.

Her world: her aunt's bakery, Sunday church at St. Nicholas, borscht arguments (everyone's grandmother's recipe is the only correct one), her mother and grandmother in Lviv on video calls every night. Married life: Christmas Eve with twelve dishes, you sitting in silence before every trip, her grandmother finally coming to Chicago; quiet ending: Sunday morning in the bakery, you fix the same door, she shakes your hand inside the doorway.
