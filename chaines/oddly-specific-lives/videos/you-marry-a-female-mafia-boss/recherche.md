# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Marry a Female Mafia Boss

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

VERIFIED FACTS about her world (use several, rounded, each inside a scene, with the source named in the sentence; never invent other statistics):
- Researchers at the University of Bath who studied the Neapolitan Camorra from 2000 to 2014 found that women took over clan leadership mostly when the men were arrested or killed: the clan used them as a "reserve army" in times of crisis, not as a sign of emancipation.
- Unlike the Sicilian Mafia, the Camorra is not one pyramid but many rival family clans, each ruling a few streets or a neighbourhood.
- Neapolitan press and Italian media call these women "madrine" (godmothers) when they lead a clan.
- "Omerta" is the code of silence: nobody talks to the police, nobody saw anything.
- The "caffe sospeso" (suspended coffee) is a real Neapolitan custom: you pay for two coffees and leave one for a stranger who cannot afford it.
- Via San Gregorio Armeno is the street of Christmas nativity-scene makers; Neapolitan Sunday lunch is a long ragu that simmers for hours.
