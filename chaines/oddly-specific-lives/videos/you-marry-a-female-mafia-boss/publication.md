## Description

Your stolen scooter comes back with an apology note. Then an entire Neapolitan street starts watching you around her.

This is Naples. This is family, favors, omerta, and the woman who asks you to stay properly. You meet Susi in the Quartieri Spagnoli, follow her through Via Toledo and Spaccanapoli, and learn what her family means when they say you are not a hobby.

A blue scooter key becomes a proposal. Then a church in the old center, two empty chairs, tarantella, confetti, and a wedding watched by the whole street.
