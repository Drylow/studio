## Description

You come to Rio for a two-year project. Then Camila's family adds you to a group chat before you've learned how to survive a churrasco.

You meet her on Copacabana Beach, cross Guanabara Bay for Sunday lunch in Niteroi, learn the rules of Brazilian football, jump seven waves on New Year's Eve, carry samba costume pieces near the Sambadrome, and propose above the city on Sugarloaf Mountain. Then the wedding fills a Catholic church with everyone who watched you try.
