# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Have to Survive a Zombie Apocalypse (Locked in a Hospital)

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

SERIES
New Oddly Specific Lives series: an ordinary guy has to survive a zombie apocalypse, day by day, in one oddly specific place.
He moves through many rooms of the place and escapes at the end. 25-30 minutes. This is episode 1.

STORY SHAPE
- Setting: Mercy Ridge General, a fictional mid-size hospital in an unnamed American city (never a real hospital name,
  no brand names). His room is Room 112 on the GROUND floor, surgical recovery ward: one bed, a window that looks onto a
  small city park with a pond and the city skyline behind it.
- You: late twenties, works night shifts at a warehouse, alone in the city, had his appendix removed. Day 1 starts the
  morning after the surgery: a juice box with one straw on the tray, a get-well card from his sister Nadia (she lives in
  another state and keeps calling until the network dies), a patient wristband.
- The infected: slow, drawn to noise and light, never fast. They gather in the park outside his window.
- Companion: Mo, the hospital's night janitor, about sixty, calm, knows every key, the basement, the laundry chute and
  the generator. Met around Day 4. Friendship only. Mo survives.
- Places to travel through (each with a fixed layout, reused when you come back): Room 112, the ground-floor corridor,
  the nurses' station, the cafeteria, the pharmacy, stairwell B, the laundry room and the basement, the generator room,
  the parking garage, the radiology wing, the roof with the helipad, and finally the park itself.
- Problems to solve day by day: water, food (vending machines, cafeteria cold room), light, the generator running out,
  his healing stitches, noise, doors and keys, radio news (an emergency broadcast loop, then a military frequency).
- The window: checked again and again. Day 1 sunny park; then the first infected; the city lights going out; fires on
  the skyline; finally soldiers setting up a landing zone in the park.
- Ending (Day 30): the military evacuates survivors from the park at dawn; You and Mo cross the park and get on the
  helicopter. Last quiet scene in a safe zone: a phone call that finally gets through to Nadia, and he still has the
  get-well card. The last image returns to the window/juice box motif.

RULES
- Day headings: "Day N: <label>", Day 1 to Day 30, increasing.
- Strictly non-graphic: no blood, no gore, no bites shown, no wounds described in detail. Danger through sounds, doors,
  shadows, windows and close calls.
- No guns-blazing heroics; ordinary, realistic solutions. No real hospital, company or brand names. No precise
  real-world statistics.
- Every object he moves, breaks, takes or loses stays that way until a scene changes it; say so in the narration.
