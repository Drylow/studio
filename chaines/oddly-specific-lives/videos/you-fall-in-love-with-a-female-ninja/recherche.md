# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Fall in Love With a Female Ninja

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

VERIFIED FACTS (use several, rounded, inside scenes, source named in the sentence; never invent other statistics):
- Iga and Koka (Koga), two mountain provinces east of Kyoto, were famous for "shinobi" (ninja), men hired for spying, infiltration and night raids during the Warring States (Sengoku) period (Britannica; Mie University's International Ninja Research Center).
- In 1581 the warlord Oda Nobunaga invaded Iga with a huge army and devastated the province (the Tensho Iga War) (Britannica; Japanese chronicles).
- The Bansenshukai, the most famous ninja manual, was compiled in Iga in 1676 (Mie University ninja research).
- "Kunoichi", the word for a female ninja, appears in Edo-period writings; historians say real evidence for female ninja is thin and much of the image comes from later stories and films (Mie University researchers). Present our heroine as the story's fiction, and this as what historians say.
- Ninja did not wear black pyjamas: historians say they dressed as farmers, monks, merchants or travelling performers to blend in; the black costume comes from kabuki theatre stagehands (Mie University; Britannica).
