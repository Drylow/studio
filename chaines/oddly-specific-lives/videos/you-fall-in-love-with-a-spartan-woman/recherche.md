# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Fall in Love With a Spartan Woman

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

VERIFIED FACTS (use several, rounded, inside scenes, source named in the sentence; never invent other statistics):
- Unlike most Greek girls, Spartan girls trained outdoors in running, wrestling, discus and javelin (Plutarch, Life of Lycurgus; Xenophon, Constitution of the Spartans).
- Aristotle wrote in his Politics that women owned about two-fifths of all the land in Sparta.
- Plutarch's "Sayings of Spartan Women" records a mother handing her son his shield with the words "With it, or on it".
- Kyniska, a Spartan princess, became the first woman to win at the Olympic Games, as owner of the winning chariot team in 396 BC (Pausanias). Real person: only mentioned, never a character.
- In 480 BC, King Leonidas and his 300 Spartans fought at Thermopylae against the Persian army (Herodotus). Leonidas only mentioned, never a character.
- Sparta's farms were worked by helots, an enslaved population that greatly outnumbered the Spartan citizens (Herodotus; Britannica).
