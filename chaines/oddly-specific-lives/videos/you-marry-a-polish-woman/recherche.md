# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Marry a Polish Woman

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

Warm culture premise: the stakes live in her family, her customs and the distance, never in stereotypes. She is a specific, competent Polish woman who chooses you; no mail-order-bride or passport-bro framing. Use real Polish details correctly (for example Wigilia with the empty seat and the opłatek wafer, pierogi, imieniny name days, the bread-and-salt welcome, oczepiny at the wedding, a grandmother who feeds everyone, real places like Kraków, Warsaw, Zakopane or Gdańsk). Affectionate and funny, never mocking. Advertiser-friendly.
