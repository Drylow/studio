## Description

She lets you into the family fortress one dumpling, toast, and impossible dance at a time.
Formal politeness comes first. Then pierogi, kapcie, Babcia's silent judgment, Marek's nalewka, and the slow realization that Polish warmth doesn't always announce itself softly.

This is Kraków in the slush, Zakopane in the snow, a Polish wedding with bread, salt, vodka, Oczepiny, Poprawiny, and a family that tests you before it keeps you.
