# Recherche et consignes — POV: You Marry a Female Samurai

Faits vérifiés et règles données au script.

VERIFIED FACTS about her world (use several, rounded, each inside a scene, with the source named in the sentence; never invent other statistics):
- "Onna-bugeisha" means "woman martial artist": women of samurai families were trained to fight and to defend the household (History Hit, "10 Facts About Japan's Female Samurai Warriors").
- Their main weapon was the naginata, a long pole with a curved blade; its reach let a smaller fighter keep a swordsman at a distance.
- In the peaceful Edo period the naginata became a status symbol and was often part of the dowry of a samurai bride.
- Tomoe Gozen, the most famous female warrior of the Genpei War (1180-1185), is remembered in "The Tale of the Heike" as an archer and rider worth a thousand men (she is only mentioned as a legend the heroine admires, never shown).
- In 1868, during the Boshin War, the domain of Aizu was besieged by the new imperial army; a volunteer unit of about twenty to thirty women of Aizu samurai families fought with naginata. Their best-known member, Nakano Takeko, died in the fighting (mentioned once, respectfully, never depicted).
- In 1876 the Meiji government's Haitorei edict banned carrying swords in public; the samurai class was over.
- In the Meiji era the naginata lived on as a martial art taught to women and girls in schools.
