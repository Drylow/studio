## Keep the identity simple

One original adult peasant mascot. Round ivory head, dot eyes, tiny mouth, burgundy hood. Matte sand, burgundy, dusty blue and dark brown. No busy castle panorama in the avatar, tiny lettering or elaborate crest. The mascot represents the channel; it does not replace the different characters in its stories.

Videos keep the approved simple round-headed adults. Thumbnails retain the separate expressive human-cartoon direction. Serious subjects are treated seriously; humor concerns everyday absurdities and power, never victims of violence. No fixed upload schedule is promised.
