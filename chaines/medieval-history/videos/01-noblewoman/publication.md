A fine dress. A family name. A room full of people negotiating your future. Follow a fictional noblewoman in fourteenth-century France as a marriage proposal becomes a lesson in family pressure, household management, property and the choices she can still make.

Based on historical scholarship. Alix and her household are fictional composites; their conversations illustrate social pressures, rather than quoting a surviving historical case.
