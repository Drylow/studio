## Description

Your job is to make powerful people laugh. The trouble starts when nobody does. Follow a fictional court jester through rehearsals, awkward silences and a performance that depends on reading the room.

Inspired by research on the medieval fool and court patronage. Colin and his household are fictional; the jokes and conversations are illustrative, rather than recorded events.
