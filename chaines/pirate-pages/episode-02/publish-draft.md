# Pirate Pages — episode 02 publishing draft

**Title (fixed):** Why Being a Pirate Captain Was Worse Than You Think

## Description (English)

You finally got the magnificent coat, and then your own crew made you buy it. Step into the boots of an imagined pirate captain in the Caribbean around 1717, sailing with Tom from our first episode, and discover what the job really involved: elections, a quartermaster who could overrule you, a cabin anyone could walk into, two shares of not very much, careening on hidden beaches, and a king who put a price on your head. This illustrated story draws on Captain Charles Johnson's General History of the Pyrates (used critically), William Snelgrave's eyewitness account, and research from Royal Museums Greenwich, the Smithsonian and North Carolina historians. The captain and crew are fictional, but piracy harmed real people, including the enslaved Africans whom pirates rarely freed. Subscribe to Pirate Pages for another life behind the legends.
