TITLE
Why Being a Pirate Was Worse Than You Think

DESCRIPTION — COPY THIS BLOCK
You wanted gold and freedom. You got a bucket.

Follow Tom, an imagined sailor in the Caribbean around 1715, as he joins a pirate crew and discovers the work behind the legend: hard food, exhausting watches, uncertain prizes, crew agreements, repairs, and the growing danger of being hunted.

The magnificent coat belongs to somebody else. Your share still has to pay for dinner.

This is an original illustrated historical scenario, informed by maritime museum research. Tom and his crew are fictional; their experiences illustrate the period rather than a real pirate's biography. Ships, agreements and circumstances varied, and piracy harmed the people whose ships and belongings were taken.
