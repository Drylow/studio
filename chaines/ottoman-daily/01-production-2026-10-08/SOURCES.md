# Ottoman 01 Research Review

Later-sixteenth-century Istanbul, approximately 1570. Illustrative leather
craft worker and household. No real individual is portrayed as a character.

- [Metropolitan Museum, age of Suleyman](https://www.metmuseum.org/TOAH/HD/suly/hd_suly.htm): building and material-culture context. Do not portray Suleyman as reigning in 1570 or show an 18th-century fountain.
- [Ottoman guilds in the early modern era, International Review of Social History](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-review-of-social-history/article/ottoman-guilds-in-the-early-modern-era/DBB9453C3BE34AEE2D4F8D3C9E390BAA): crafts and changing regulation. No exact 18th-century shoemakers' hierarchy transplanted into this workshop.
- [Hacettepe University, bakers in Rodoscuk, 1546-1552](https://research.hacettepe.edu.tr/en/publications/becoming-a-baker-in-the-ottoman-town-of-rodos%C3%A7uk-1546-1552-a-text/): changing craft organization within the century. Evidence is from Rodoscuk, not the diary of an Istanbul baker; narration says Ottoman towns.
- [Istanbul fountains, architectural research](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/1620038): public and institutional water structures. A modest period-appropriate access point is staged, not a named later monument.
- [Yavuz Sultan Selim foundation's 1538 imaret register](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/osmed/article/1797849): specified food beneficiaries and administrative labor. No universal free-food entitlement inferred.
- [Istanbul coffee roasting and trade research](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/mtad/article/1029211): sixteenth-century rise of coffeehouses; later administrative detail from 1590 onward is not the protagonist's 1570 routine.
- [Historical Ottoman bathhouse study](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/2272632): historical bathing context. Only the clothed entrance is shown; no copied modern reception, shower or timetable.

## Boundaries

No fez, baroque 18th-century fountains, later industrial harbor equipment,
universal coffeehouse attendance, fixed invented shop hours, exact repair
prices, universal imaret meals, or everyone represented as socially equal.
Clothing is a modest period-inspired adaptation to the covered white-headed
adult style. Do not add giant invented minarets to a modest workshop skyline.
Personal repair requests, meetings, household supplies and route are illustrative.
