# Tang 01 Research Review

Chang'an around 750 CE, before the An Lushan rebellion and ninth-century
religious persecutions. Not Song Kaifeng or Qing Beijing. Illustrative pottery
business and household, not a documented historical individual's diary.

- [Guimet Museum, Tang exhibition](https://www.guimet.fr/fr/expositions/la-chine-des-tang): cosmopolitan material culture, everyday occupations and musical evidence.
- [Guimet Museum, exhibition press release](https://www.guimet.fr/sites/default/files/2024-11/cp-la-chine-des-tang.pdf): capital and artistic context. The much larger press dossier could not be read through the web extractor; no claim of having read that dossier.
- [Columbia University, Tang dynasty presentation](https://video.afe.easia.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/The-Tang-Dynasty-in-China-Presentation-PDF.pdf): capital plan and two principal markets.
- [Cardiff University research, city and ward organization](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/55826/1/U584361.pdf): walls, access and night restrictions. No exact opening or curfew minute is narrated; later changes must not be treated as one fixed dynasty-wide schedule.
- [Metropolitan Museum, Tang internationalism](https://www.metmuseum.org/zh/essays/internationalism-in-the-tang-dynasty-618-906): cultural, commercial and religious connections. The 842-846 persecution is outside this episode's period.
- [Smithsonian, Tang riding figures](https://asia-archive.si.edu/learn/for-educators/teaching-china-with-the-smithsonian/explore-by-object/tomb-figures-of-a-man-and-woman-on-horseback/): funerary context; not a direct view of a typical kitchen.
- [Metropolitan Museum, early-eighth-century rider](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/44805): horse imagery, transport and rank. No universal mounted commuting.

## Boundaries

No paper currency, later Song night-market routine, Qing queue hairstyles,
Ming porcelain fashion used as a Tang household norm, or three-color burial
figures presented as everybody's everyday tableware. Avoid invented daily
prices, precise neighborhood names, exact transit route and universal menus.
Personal deliveries, purchases and conversation are illustrative. New pottery
at the forming stage is not today's already-fired delivery stock.
