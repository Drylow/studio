TITLE
What Did People in Edo Japan Actually Do All Day?

DESCRIPTION
What filled an ordinary day in Edo, the city that would become Tokyo?
Follow an imagined artisan household around 1700: folding away the bedding,
fetching water, eating breakfast, fixing a troublesome wooden box, running
errands, handling small coins, and finding a little time for the bathhouse,
street entertainment, and a quiet cup of tea.

This is a source-backed illustrated exploration, not a recovered diary.
Our unnamed household and its errands are fictional examples. Daily life varied
with occupation, wealth, season, neighborhood, and household circumstances.
The drawings are stylized illustrations, not exact architectural reconstructions.
