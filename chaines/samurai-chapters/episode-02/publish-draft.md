## YouTube description

Two swords, a family name, no lord, and a landlord asking who will vouch for you. What really happened to samurai who lost their masters in Tokugawa Japan? Follow Kenji, a fictional low-ranking retainer in the 1720s, through dismissal, suspicious cities, job hunting and side work, then see how the famous 47 ronin compare with ordinary ronin, right down to their leader's account book. Kenji is imagined to illustrate the period; the people, rules and events around him come from historians and Japanese reference works.
