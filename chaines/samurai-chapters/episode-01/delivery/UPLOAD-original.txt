TITLE
Why Being a Samurai Was Worse Than You Think

DESCRIPTION
Two swords. A respected family name. An empty purse.

What was everyday life really like for a samurai during Japan's long peace? Step into the world of a lower-ranking retainer around 1720, where the hardest battles involve rice stipends, debt, paperwork, family expectations, and keeping your lord happy.

This illustrated journey follows Kenji, a fictional character used to explore historical conditions in Tokugawa Japan. Samurai lives varied greatly by rank, domain, resources, and period.
