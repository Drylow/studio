## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 271 to Day 300

## DESCRIPTION

Two hard knocks at your gate in the middle of the night, and a torch beam that went straight to your vent grille. For two hundred and seventy days, you have known exactly how the dead come to your door. Now three strangers are sitting in the street, and they say they walked nineteen days to find you.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 271 to 300 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A woman in a yellow raincoat explains how she found you, an old postcard turns out to be a real place, a road to the sea closes itself twice a day, and a spring rain starts filling the gravel pit where the horde is locked away.

Would you have opened the gate to strangers? Would you leave your bunker for a rock in the sea? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 301.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 to 9: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet. Early spring: frost gone, then a week of heavy rain, then sun and steam.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack (Vera's goggles inside). Talks through the gate, makes the daylight rule, opens the gate on day 275; can't imagine the bunker empty.
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm (white paint stains), teal backpack, the flashlight, the radio at sunset. Teaches the strangers the knock; walks away fast when Ruth says they heard "Still here" at the sea; shows Ruth the locker.
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror, roll of tape in his pocket. Describes the strangers from the gantry; tapes cardboard over the vent grille; gets an oil can from Bram; takes his beanie off (third time ever) at the locker; starts a "things we'd take" list on the bunker wall.
- **Vera**: long grey scarf, brown and grey only (no goggles anymore: Patch keeps them). Holds the rope of the gate bolt from the gantry; "My favorite kind" about the Point; on her feet at once on day 300.
- **Ruth** (new): **bright yellow raincoat with the hood down**, the only yellow on screen besides Ansel's mitten. Calm, direct, kind. Walked nineteen days from the coast; draws the Point in the ash; recognizes her lighthouse on Nora's postcard.
- **Kit** (new): a boy of about twelve, **white bicycle helmet** that he never takes off. Curious; adds four marks to people met; meets the hill boy.
- **Bram** (new): very big and tall, **blue and white checked blanket worn as a cape**. Never speaks; whistles (three notes = done, two notes = not good). Mends things, oils the gate, sleeps sitting up against the gate.
- **Ansel** (radio only): "Pit's quiet. Bridge is ours." then "Pit's wet / filling / up to their waists"; on day 300, whispering.
- **The hill boy** (blue bandage) and hill people with apples (day 282).

### Creatures
