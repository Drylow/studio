# New cast — episode 3 (Days 61–90)

One new named character appears on screen in this episode: **Wes**, the man from the radio
(voice only in episodes 1 and 2). No reference image exists yet; one must be made before
production, from the description below. Every other living person stays off screen: the woman
from the truck is a voice, a hand at the cab window (day 70) or reaching out of the cab door
(day 90), and on day 90 a dark silhouette with two hands on the wheel; the people from the hill are two anonymous silhouettes on the truck roof (day 70).

## Wes

**Exact look (same stick-figure style as Patch and Nora):**
- Adult, same proportions as Patch: round **white** head, small black oval dot eyes, short black
  mouth line, thin black stick body and limbs, white mitten hands, white oval feet. **No hair**,
  no realistic face.
- **Item 1, bright red knit beanie** with a folded, ribbed brim, sitting on the top third of the
  round head. The head stays perfectly round under it. No hair shows below the brim.
- **Item 2, small round signal mirror**: a flat silver disc with a thin grey rim, about the size of
  his palm, hanging on a thin black cord around his neck and resting on the middle of his chest.
- **No backpack, no bandage, no armband.** He must never read as Patch (orange bandage and orange
  pack) or Nora (purple forearm band and teal pack).
- Colours to avoid on him: orange, purple, teal, green (walkers), navy (the night driver), olive
  (the Doorman), yellow (sign paint). Red and silver only.

**Pose and body language:** loose, a little slouched, hands often caught mid-gesture because he
talks a lot (always in a whisper). Asleep: lying on the bunker stairs with the beanie pulled down
over his eyes.

**One variant needed:** day 77, **hat off**. A plain round white head, identical to Patch's
without the bandage, holding the red beanie in one mitten hand.

**Props he carries in the episode:** a cardboard box holding a can of oil and two battery packs
(day 61); the oil can (day 61, kneeling at the gate wheel); a screwdriver and the office door
latch (days 68–69, at the bay door); the handheld two-way radio (day 76, night, at the top of the
bunker stairs); his hand on the switch of the open control box at the far end of the gantry
(day 90).

**Where he appears:** at the street gate (days 61 and 78), in the bunker (sleeping on the stairs,
reading the wall chart, eating a sugared almond), in the corridor at the bay door, on the gantry
at the control box, and climbing down the canopy ladder (day 78). At the end of day 90 he is
**inside the depot with Nora**.
