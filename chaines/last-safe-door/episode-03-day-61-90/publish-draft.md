## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 61 to Day 90

## DESCRIPTION

Somebody alive is standing at your gate, and something much bigger is coming. Thousands of the dead are walking toward town along the motorway, and the people on the hill want your yard to catch them, using the loudspeaker you once risked everything to switch off. You live directly underneath it.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 61 to 90 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. The voice on the radio finally walks through the gate, a stranger in a white truck makes an offer you can't easily refuse, and the gate that used to scream learns to whisper.

Would you have shouted? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 91.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 and 2: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet, grey concrete depot, warm lantern light below and grey daylight above.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye (he keeps the orange one when the hill sends only blue bandages), orange backpack.
- **Nora**: same style, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack, usually the one holding the flashlight.
- **Wes**: **new on screen from day 61**. See `NEW_CAST.md` (red knit beanie, small round silver mirror on a cord, no backpack). He needs a reference image before production, plus a hat-off variant for day 77.
- **The woman from the truck**: voice only. At most a hand at the cab window (day 70) or reaching out of the open cab door (day 90), and on day 90 a dark silhouette with two hands on the wheel inside the cab. Never her face.
- **People from the hill**: two anonymous silhouettes on the truck roof, tying sacks to a rope (day 70).

### Creatures
- **Walkers**: green round heads, black dot eyes, open mouths, torn olive or blue-grey clothes. Seen as the crowd at the gate (days 61, 67–79), then as **the river**: thousands filling the yard on day 90, and its tail in the street.
- **The Doorman** (reference "Cart Infected": green head, olive-gray ragged vest): the first walker at the gate from day 67. His olive sleeve is in the gap on day 78, he leaves toward the river on day 85, and on day 90 he is the last one out of the gateway behind Patch.
- **The night driver** (reference "Delivery Infected": green head, navy-blue two-pocket vest, called the "blue depot vest" in the script): **still locked in the coach** all episode. He stands at the front of the aisle facing Nora (day 66) and presses against the glass when Patch crawls on the canopy roof (day 80).
- **The eleven passengers**: walkers seen through the coach windows. On day 80 they look up at the roof, and on day 90 they are surrounded by the river.

### Props
- White box truck, with its cab door swinging open (day 90).
- Cardboard box with a can of oil and two battery packs; Wes pouring oil on the gate wheel.
- Handheld two-way radio, flashlight, lantern.
- Pencil wall chart with **four columns**: days, walkers gone, people met (one mark), and days until the river.
- Tiny white tulle bags of sugared almonds.
- The office door latch and a screwdriver; the repaired bay door latch.
- Rope lowered from the gantry, sacks, tins, crackers, ration packs, four big water jugs, battery packs, a second oil can, a first-aid kit of **blue** bandages.
- Suitcases from the coach's luggage hold, stacked behind the desk and squeaky trolley.
- Long aluminium **canopy ladder** lashed to the far end of the gantry, reaching down into the yard beside the gate.
- Cardboard sign: "TWO" crossed out, **"THREE ALIVE"** plus the radio channel, in yellow bus-bay paint.
- Steel bar wedged in the gate rail (days 79–89).
- Control box with a small battery light, half green (day 80) and then fully green (day 83); the switch.
- Cloth and a water bottle for the solar panel.

### Locations (7 sets + 2 background plates)
1. **Bunker**: concrete room, stairs up to the steel door (Wes sleeps on them), bench, racks now refilled, lantern, wall chart.
2. **Upstairs service corridor**: the bay door with its new latch, then sealed behind the desk, trolley and a wall of suitcases (from day 71).
3. **Maintenance stairs and gantry**: the control box at the far end, the sign hanging over the street, and the canopy ladder lashed at the far end.
4. **Canopy roof**: thin metal roof with the solar panel and the loudspeaker horn, the coach directly below (new set, day 80).
5. **Depot yard and street gate**: the oiled sliding gate, its bolt and rail, and the **narrow slot** between the open panel and the wall. Empty until day 90, then packed with the river from the canopy almost to the gate.
6. **Coach**: exterior with its folding door (Nora sitting on the concrete beside it), and walkers seen through the windows.
7. **Street outside the gate** (new set at street level): parked cars, a side road and the white truck. On day 90 at dusk, it is full of the river's tail.
8. **Truck cab at dusk** (background or short insert): dark cab, a silhouette driver, and the view back to the depot wall with the sign and one small light blinking on the gantry.
9. **View from the gantry** (background only): rooftops, and far off a dark line of the river on the road beyond the hill (day 85).
