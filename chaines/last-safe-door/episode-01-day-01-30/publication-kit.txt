Zombie Bunker Survival: 30 Days Under a Zombie Horde

DESCRIPTION
You're hiding beneath a bus depot. Hundreds of zombies are walking across your ceiling. The steel door buys you time, but dwindling supplies, a squeaky trolley, and a loudspeaker that won't shut up keep spending it.

Follow Patch and Nora through 30 days underground in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. Every sound has consequences. Every trip upstairs is a risk.
