The gate at the end of the road to the Point is leaning, and in five weeks the lowest tide of the year will leave that road dry for half a day. The depot is full of the dead, your bunker is empty, and seven people need to cross two days of roads nobody has driven in a year. There is only one vehicle big enough, and it is standing in the mud at the bottom of a gravel pit.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 331 to 360 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A travel alarm clock does its second job, a good bus wakes up for the last time in the pit, a lamp leaves its window, Nora repaints the sign, and the road to the sea finally starts.
