## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 331 to Day 360

## DESCRIPTION

The gate at the end of the road to the Point is leaning, and in five weeks the lowest tide of the year will leave that road dry for half a day. The depot is full of the dead, your bunker is empty, and seven people need to cross two days of roads nobody has driven in a year. There is only one vehicle big enough, and it is standing in the mud at the bottom of a gravel pit.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 331 to 360 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A travel alarm clock does its second job, a good bus wakes up for the last time in the pit, a lamp leaves its window, Nora repaints the sign, and the road to the sea finally starts.

Would you risk the broken bridge, or take the long way round? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 361.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 to 11: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet. Late spring: green fields, dry cracked mud in the pit, then flat salty country and a wide brown river near the coast.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack (Vera's goggles inside). "Then we'd better go and get it"; unbolts the pit gate with Bram; holds Nora's ladder; drives the white truck behind the coach.
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack (postcard and road map), the flashlight. Asks Ansel to come; tells the Doorman "Look after it"; paints SEVEN ALIVE. GONE TO THE SEA. on the sign; "I'm going anyway"; rides in the truck.
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror, roll of tape. Jump leads; the list ("Things we'd take"); rides beside Vera in the coach.
- **Vera**: long grey scarf, brown and grey only. Uses the travel alarm clock to lure the stuck dead and leaves it hanging in the pit; drives the coach out of the pit ("Good bus") and to the sea; receives Ansel's lamp.
- **Ruth**: bright yellow raincoat. Guides from the coach with the pencil line on the map.
- **Kit**: boy of about twelve, white bicycle helmet, **now with the hill boy's blue bandage tied round the helmet strap**. Stops sleeping when the radio goes quiet; hears Tam at midnight on day 359.
- **Bram**: very big, blue and white checked blanket worn as a cape. Cleans the mud out of the coach engine; says his first word ("Again."); loads the four barrier sections "in case anything needs a gate."
- **Ansel**: long coat, one yellow and one green mitten. Fills the coach with the city's last diesel; gives Vera the lamp from the window; stays: "Somebody has to keep the bridge."
- **The city people** (the girl with the funnel and handcart), **the hill people**, **the hill boy** (gives his blue bandage to Kit).
- **Tam** (voice only, on the radio): Kit's father at the lighthouse.
