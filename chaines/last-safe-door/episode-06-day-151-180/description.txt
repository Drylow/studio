Winter has come to the bus depot. The yard is full, the street is full, the food is almost gone, and the only way out is the coach parked in the middle of a few thousand of the dead. It needs three things to drive: a charged battery, an empty driver's seat, and the keys that are still in its dead driver's pocket.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 151 to 180 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. The frost slows the dead down, a hole opens in the canopy roof, and Nora finally tells you what the night driver used to say to her every night for nine years.

Would you have gone down through the hatch? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 181.
