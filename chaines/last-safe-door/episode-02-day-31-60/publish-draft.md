## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 31 to Day 60

## DESCRIPTION

A truck dragged part of the horde out of the bus depot. The rest is still on your ceiling. The food is running out, and the next supply room is a coach parked in the middle of the yard. Every sound still brings them. This time, you learn to make them go where you want.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 31 to 60 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A voice on the radio finally has a name, an open gate keeps letting the dead back in, and a bag of empty cans becomes the most valuable thing you own.

Would you open the gate? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 61.

Style unchanged from episode 1: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet, grey concrete depot, warm lantern light below and grey daylight above.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage across the forehead (a fresh one from day 39 that looks identical), orange backpack.
- **Nora**: same style, purple band on one forearm, teal backpack, usually the one holding the flashlight.
- **Wes**: voice only, on the radio. Do not draw him in this episode.
- **Truck driver (woman)**: voice only. At most a living hand at the cab window of the white truck, as in episode 1.

### Creatures
- **Walkers**: green round heads, black dot eyes, open mouths, torn olive or blue-grey clothes. Seen as a crowd in the yard, then pressed against the street gate.
- **Walker in a blue depot vest**: the same zombie design with a blue depot work vest. He was the night-route driver, and he boards the coach last.
- **The eleven "passengers"**: walkers seen through the coach windows from day 55 on.
