For ninety days, you hid under a bus depot. Now you are on the outside of it, in a truck with a stranger, leading the tail of a horde through streets you have never seen. Your friends are locked in under thousands of the dead, and the only way back in is two kilometers on foot and a rope over the wall.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 91 to 120 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. The woman from the truck finally shows her face, the hill turns out to have a name for your home, and a light blinks across the whole town every hour, just to say still here.

Would you have walked home? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 121.
