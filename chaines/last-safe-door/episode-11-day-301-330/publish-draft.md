## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 301 to Day 330

## DESCRIPTION

The gravel pit had one way out, until the rain gave it a second. Now the horde you buried is standing under the windows of nineteen people in the city, with their water on the other side, and nowhere in the world is far enough away. Except one place you know can hold every one of them: your own yard.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 301 to 330 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A strip of tape finally comes off a switch, the bunker is emptied for the first time, the whole horde follows one white truck home, the loudspeaker makes its last announcement, and the Doorman has to decide which side of the gate he belongs on.

Would you have given your home to the horde? Would you go to the sea, or stay on the hill? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 331.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 to 10: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet. Spring: mud turning green at the edges, sunny days, cold nights.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack (Vera's goggles inside). Says "Here" (the yard); drives the white truck to the city and leads the horde home with the horn; lets the truck roll silently up the hill road; says "Now" on the radio.
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack (postcard and road map at the bottom), the flashlight. Turns the lantern off for the first time (day 305); holds the gate rope on the gantry; knocks three times and once softer on the gate to bring the Doorman in; "Welcome home."
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror, roll of tape. Peels the tape off the loudspeaker switch and keeps it on his sleeve; copies the chart totals onto cardboard ("Moved house, day 305"); talks to the horde through the loudspeaker until he loses his voice; crawls along the canopy roof to switch it off; tapes the gate bolt shut.
- **Vera**: long grey scarf, brown and grey only. Rides in the truck; finds the travel alarm clock in the field (day 310) and winds it; says "Still here" on the radio for the first time; sleeps lying down for the first time (day 327).
- **Ruth**: bright yellow raincoat. Stays on the hill; calls the Point every night with no answer; on day 330, Tam (Kit's father) answers.
- **Kit**: boy of about twelve, white bicycle helmet. Reads an old bus timetable into the radio for the loudspeaker; sits by the radio at night; hears his father first.
- **Bram**: very big, blue and white checked blanket worn as a cape, never speaks, whistles (three notes done, two not good, two then a third: maybe). Rides in the truck; fixes the coughing engine with tape; props a ladder on the street side of the depot wall.
- **Ansel** (in the top window, then on the radio): long coat, one yellow and one green mitten; the lamp is lit again; "Whose home?"; "Bridge is ours. Again."
- **Tam** (new, voice only): Kit's father (so Ruth is Kit's mother), keeper of the lighthouse at the Point. Never shown in this episode.
- **The hill people**: handcarts on the hill road; give the seven a long room with big windows.

### Creatures
- **The river**: filling the bridge, the slip road and the city streets; turning "like the tide"; following the truck down the motorway; filling the yard wall to wall like a bathtub; faces turned up to the loudspeaker; still and silent after the click.
- **The night driver** (navy two-pocket depot vest) and **the Doorman** (olive vest): at the front of the horde; the Doorman stops in the gateway with both hands on the gate, then walks in after Nora's knock.
