The gravel pit had one way out, until the rain gave it a second. Now the horde you buried is standing under the windows of nineteen people in the city, with their water on the other side, and nowhere in the world is far enough away. Except one place you know can hold every one of them: your own yard.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 301 to 330 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A strip of tape finally comes off a switch, the bunker is emptied for the first time, the whole horde follows one white truck home, the loudspeaker makes its last announcement, and the Doorman has to decide which side of the gate he belongs on.
