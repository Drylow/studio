## DESCRIPTION

Your night shift is over when a patient's hand slides across the glass of your ward door. The emergency desk doesn't answer, the fire doors close, and you are a night porter who knows every corridor of the hospital because you push beds through them all night.

Follow a night porter and Dana, the nurse who locks ward 4B behind you, through 100 days inside a dead hospital in an original animated zombie survival movie told from your point of view. A generator that dies on Day 6, a lift bell that calls the infected, a linen chute, a radio on the roof and an ambulance that has to start at dawn.

Would you have stayed on ward 4B or gone down to the basement? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for the next story.
