## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 361 to Day 390

## DESCRIPTION

Halfway across the last bridge before the sea, a piece of the road has fallen into the river, and what is left is barely wider than the coach. On the other side, the gate at the end of the causeway is gone, thirty-one people are trapped around a lighthouse, and in five days the lowest tide of the year will leave the road to them dry for half a day.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 361 to 390 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. Four sections of motorway barrier become a bridge and then a gate, a postcard comes true, a good bus blows its horn one last time across a bay, and the sea closes a door that will stay shut for a whole year.

Would you have driven the bus onto the sand? Who do you think is out on the water? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 391.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 to 12: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet. Early summer by the sea: pale sand, flat shining water, wet sand flats at low tide, gulls, stone houses.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack (Vera's goggles, now also the postcard). Holds down a barrier section; drives the truck across the broken bridge; turns the stragglers with the truck horn on day 365; on watch with Kit on day 390.
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack, the flashlight, old cleaning shoes. Holds up the postcard to the real view; says "We're the depot"; writes "Still here" on the back of the postcard and gives it to Patch; says Still here into the radio every sunset.
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror (**lost in the sea on day 365**), roll of tape. Guides the coach and the truck across the bridge with the mirror; tapes the horn on the Hump; escapes with Vera by the roof hatch; takes his beanie off on the beach (fourth time).
- **Vera**: long grey scarf, brown and grey only. Walks the bridge; drives the coach across with two fingers; leads the dead onto the Hump; smiles (second time); lights Ansel's lamp in the lighthouse window.
- **Ruth**: bright yellow raincoat. Flashes the lighthouse from the coach roof; "That's our knock."
- **Kit**: boy of about twelve, white bicycle helmet with the hill boy's blue bandage on the strap. Runs into the sea to his father.
- **Bram**: very big, blue and white checked blanket worn as a cape. Weighs down a barrier section; bolts the four sections across the causeway with the Point people; oils the new gate and sleeps against it.
- **Tam** (first on screen): Kit's father, **dark green oilskin coat and dark green fisherman's hat**. Rows the small wooden boat; draws the bay in the wet sand with an oar; picks Vera and Wes up from the rocks.
- **The Point people** (31 including Tam): fishermen and families, nets, a harbor with six boats.
- **Ansel** (voice only, two words on the radio): "Bridge's ours."

### Creatures

- **The horde on the sand**: hundreds following the coach across the bay and onto the Hump; left there, around the coach, for a year.
- **A few stragglers** on the beach who only follow the second time.
