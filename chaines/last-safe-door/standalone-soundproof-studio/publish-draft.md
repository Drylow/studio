## TITLE

Zombie Outbreak: 30 Days Trapped In A Soundproof Studio

## DESCRIPTION

At 2:14 in the morning, the city above starts screaming, and you are standing in the one room built to keep every sound out: a soundproof recording studio in a basement downtown. The dead hunt by sound. They can't hear you in here. But you can't hear them either.

Follow Patch, a night sound engineer, and Nora, a singer recording her first demo, through 30 days behind a soundproof door in an original animated zombie survival story told from your point of view. A microphone taped in the stairwell becomes your only ears, a drummer's old recording clears the street, a rainstorm swells the door seal open, and in the end the only way out is the loudest song the city has ever heard.

Would you have stayed behind the soundproof door, or walked out with the song? What would you have played to the dead? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for the next story.
