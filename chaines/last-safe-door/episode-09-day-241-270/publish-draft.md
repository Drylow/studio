## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 241 to Day 270

## DESCRIPTION

The horde you sent down the motorway didn't disappear. It is standing on a bridge at the edge of the city, and the people who live there have been whispering under it for a month. Now they are on your radio, asking for the driver. Your yard is empty and your gate is your own, but the rule never said what to do when you are the one who sent them.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 241 to 270 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A lamp in one window finally gets explained, the last bus wakes up for one more journey, a gravel pit becomes a place with one way out, and four sections of motorway barrier become the first gate you ever closed with everyone on the right side of it.

Would you have gone back for the river? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 271.

## CHARACTERS, CREATURES, PROPS AND LOCATIONS NEEDED

Style unchanged from episodes 1 to 8: white round heads, black dot eyes, thin black stick bodies, white mitten hands and feet. Late winter daylight: frost on the roads and fields, pale sky.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack. Drives the white truck with the barrier sections; hauls the rope at the pit; keeps Vera's goggles in the orange bag (from day 266; he does not wear them).
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm (white paint stains), teal backpack, the flashlight. Outside the walls again; on the rim of the pit with the flashlight and the rope; pins the road map inside her open locker beside the beach postcard.
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror, a roll of tape in his pocket. **Comes on the trip this time**; jump-starts the coach with the truck; rides in the coach and tapes the horn down at the bottom of the pit; climbs the rope; adds nineteen marks to people met.
- **Vera**: brown driving goggles (pulled down to drive, then given to Patch on day 266), long grey scarf, brown and grey only. Answers the radio ("This is the driver"); drives the coach one last time; climbs out of the roof hatch and up the rope; sleeps sitting up with the door open.
- **Ansel** (new, the voice from the city): tall and thin, long coat, **one yellow mitten and one green one**. Calm, tired, polite. Kept the lamp in the window for Vera.
- **The city people** (new): nineteen in all, never named; on screen, a woman and a girl pulling a handcart of fuel cans, then a few more with spanners, buckets, and one man singing badly on the bridge.
- **The hill people**: two grown-ups and **the boy with the blue bandage**, who keep the depot (days 245–262) and knock three times and once softer from the inside.
- **Unseen visitors** (day 270): boots in the street, a torch beam through the grille, two hard knocks. Never shown.

### Creatures

- **The night driver** (navy two-pocket depot vest) and **the Doorman** (olive vest): the last two down the road into the pit, side by side.
