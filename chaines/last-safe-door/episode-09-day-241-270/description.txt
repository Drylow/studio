The horde you sent down the motorway didn't disappear. It is standing on a bridge at the edge of the city, and the people who live there have been whispering under it for a month. Now they are on your radio, asking for the driver. Your yard is empty and your gate is your own, but the rule never said what to do when you are the one who sent them.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 241 to 270 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A lamp in one window finally gets explained, the last bus wakes up for one more journey, a gravel pit becomes a place with one way out, and four sections of motorway barrier become the first gate you ever closed with everyone on the right side of it.
