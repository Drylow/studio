## DESCRIPTION

Flight 417 is still boarding when the man in the airport uniform turns toward the glass at gate B12. Your shift is over, your badge still works, and the only way out you know runs through the baggage tunnels under the terminal.

Follow a night baggage handler, June, a stubborn retired teacher with a suitcase full of red wool, Malik, a controller stuck in the tower, and Captain Ines, a cargo pilot with a plane and no fuel, through 100 days inside a dead airport in an original animated zombie survival movie told from your point of view. A badge reader that turns red, a belt that starts on its own, a fuel truck by the hangar, and one last departure over the fence.
