## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 121 to Day 150

## DESCRIPTION

You called the horde away from the hill, and now it is standing outside your gate. The yard is full, the street is full, and the bus depot has become an island in a sea of the dead. From now on, every meal has to cross the street on a rope, high above thousands of upturned faces.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 121 to 150 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. The loudspeaker calls the dead one more time, the gutters Nora hated for nine years finally pay her back, and the woman who never goes into places with one way out runs out of choices.

Would you have turned the speaker on? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 151.
