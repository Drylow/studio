## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 181 to Day 210

## DESCRIPTION

The coach has its keys and a full battery. Now eleven passengers have to get off, a few thousand of the dead have to follow it out of the gate, and the woman from the truck has to drive the last bus out of the depot with the whole horde behind her. If it works, the yard will be empty for the first time since day one.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 181 to 210 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. The loudspeaker makes its last announcement, Nora says goodbye to the night driver, and a radio voice grows fainter every sunset, all the way down the motorway.

Would you have opened the gate? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 211.
