## TITLE

Zombie Bunker Survival: Day 211 to Day 240

## DESCRIPTION

The last bus out of the depot ran out of fuel on the motorway, with the whole horde standing around it and the woman who drove it trapped inside. Your yard is empty for the first time since day one, your truck has no battery, and Nora has never set foot outside the depot walls. Somebody has to go and get her.

Follow Patch and Nora through days 211 to 240 in an original animated survival story told from your point of view. A hill starts a dead engine, a horde follows a horn down a motorway, a travel alarm clock does one last job, and the woman from the truck finally says her name.

Would you have gone to the motorway? Which decision would you have made differently? Tell us in the comments, and subscribe to Last Safe Door for day 241.

### Characters
- **Patch** (the "you"): white round head, orange bandage above the right eye, orange backpack (between his feet in the cab). **Drives the white truck** from day 213.
- **Nora**: purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack, the flashlight. **Outside the depot walls for the first time** (day 214); in the truck cab; knocking at the gate three times and once softer (day 219); painting FOUR on the sign (white paint on her purple band); opening her locker (day 228).
- **Wes**: red knit beanie, small round silver mirror, no backpack. Explains the clutch through the truck window; **stays to keep the depot** (days 214–218), flashing the mirror from the gantry; opening the gate with his arms out; hat off, held to his chest (day 219); taping the speaker switch on OFF.
- **Vera** (the woman from the truck, **named from day 215**): brown driving goggles, long grey scarf, brown and grey only (sheet in `../episode-04-day-91-120/CONTINUITY_DAY120.md`).
