## Description

The funeral bill is the one families fear, but the bigger bill usually arrives years earlier in medical statements.

This video follows one typical American case: a widowed retiree in Pennsylvania, late 70s, a paid-off house, five years of health decline, hospice, a funeral with viewing and burial, and probate. We count the family-facing tab, then separate it from the public bill Medicare pays in the background.

More bills people don't explain clearly: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

We cover last-five-years medical spending, Medicare's last-year costs, long-term care, Medicaid estate recovery, hospice, funeral pricing, cremation versus burial, the FTC Funeral Rule, and the running tab that reaches $66,988 in this constructed case.
