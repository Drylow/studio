## Description

A car accident can keep billing you for three years after the bumper is fixed.

This video follows one typical low-speed crash where both cars drive away, nobody needs an ambulance, and the personal tab still reaches $5,023. We go through the ER bill, the rental car, the collision deductible, the repair claim, the other driver's property damage claim, and the premium increases that arrive later.

If this changes how you see full coverage, the next bill is here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

The numbers come from sources named in the video, including NHTSA, Peterson-KFF Health System Tracker, Enterprise Mobility, Hertz, CCC Intelligent Solutions, the Insurance Information Institute, ISO, Verisk, LendingTree, Quadrant Information Services, the City of Houston, and the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. General information only, not legal, insurance, financial, or medical advice.
