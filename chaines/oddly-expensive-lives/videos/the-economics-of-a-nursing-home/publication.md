## Description

In this case, two years of nursing home care reaches $233,856 before the house becomes the fight.

This video follows one constructed 82-year-old widow through the five handoffs that create the bill: hospital, skilled nursing rehab, private pay, Medicaid, and estate recovery. The numbers come from CMS, Medicare.gov, KFF, CareScout, Social Security, CDC, ASPE, HHS, MedPAC, and Medicaid.gov where the script names them.

If this is already changing how you see long-term care, subscribe because the next bill is worse: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

We cover the Medicare Part A deductible, skilled nursing facility copays, the day Medicare stops paying, the private-pay nursing home room, the Medicaid asset limit, the personal-needs allowance, the house exclusion, the five-year look-back, and estate recovery.
