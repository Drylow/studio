## Description

The wedding-day average is $34,000. Add the ring, rehearsal dinner, and honeymoon, and this case reaches $47,860 before hidden charges and interest.

Using The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study, this breakdown follows a couple around 30 in Virginia with 117 guests and a fall Saturday wedding. The tab moves from the engagement ring and venue to catering, photography, the dress, flowers, the DJ, wedding rings, the rehearsal dinner, and the honeymoon. It also shows what the $34,000 headline average leaves out, including alcohol, videography, a planner, cake, service charges, and tips.
