## Description

$4,613 can disappear before the car insurance company even opens the file.

This is the real bill of getting arrested for a first DUI in Volusia County, Florida, built from county fee schedules, Florida law, FLHSMV figures, Nolo's first-DUI survey, BLS wages, BJS jail data, and federal traffic safety numbers. The tab starts at the roadside with towing and storage, then moves through booking fees, jail fees, bond, missed work, a lawyer, fines, court costs, probation, DUI school, and license reinstatement.

More bills here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

This is general information, not legal or financial advice. Fees move by county, state, year, and clerk window.
