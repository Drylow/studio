## Description

The siren may be public, but the bill can act like an out-of-network medical claim.

This video follows one constructed 5-mile 911 ambulance ride, using real rounded figures from KFF, Peterson-KFF, HCCI, FAIR Health, Health Affairs, the GAO, and the Commonwealth Fund. We add the base rate, mileage, a possible out-of-network balance bill, and the emergency room out-of-pocket cost into one running tab.

More bills explained here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

The strange part is where the protection stops. The federal No Surprises Act covers air ambulances, but ground ambulances still depend heavily on ownership, insurance contracts, deductibles, and state law.
