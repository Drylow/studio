## Description

The first-year cash hit for a typical North Carolina couple reaches $28,314.

This bill starts with $2,854 in birth-related out-of-pocket costs, then adds 12 weeks of unpaid leave, diapers, daycare, and the Child Tax Credit. The tab also steps back to the USDA's $233,610 estimate and Brookings Institution's $310,605 update for raising a child through age 17, before looking at College Board's 2025 to 2026 public four-year college costs.
