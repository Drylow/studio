## Description

A divorce can settle before trial and still turn into a $61,000 household bill.

This video follows one typical contested divorce in the United States: legal fees, custody costs, second rent, selling the house, splitting a 401(k), and the trial bill they avoid by settling. The lawyer invoice is real, but the larger cost is turning one household system into two.

More bills explained here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlyExpensiveLives?sub_confirmation=1

This is general information, not legal or financial advice. Divorce costs change by state, county, income, assets, kids, and conflict level.
