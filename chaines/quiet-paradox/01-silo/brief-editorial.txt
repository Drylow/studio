STRUCTURE HINT
Twenty-two independently understandable discussions. Each opens with a concrete detail, separates the television fact from an interpretation, tests alternatives and closes its local question. The sequence expands from a single cleaner to the network's governance, material sustainability, consent, succession, communication and decisions under uncertainty, then a credible route toward freedom.
