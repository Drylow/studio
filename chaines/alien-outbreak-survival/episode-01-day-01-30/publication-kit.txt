TITLE
Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 1 to Day 30

DESCRIPTION
An alien craft arrives over the city. You find shelter at a gas station with food, water, and a door you can close. Then you notice the same patrol returning every forty minutes.

Follow Reed and Mara through the first thirty days of the invasion. Each supply run leaves a trace. Each new observation changes the plan. When the patrol stops following its old routine, the place that kept them alive becomes much harder to leave.

Why are the aliens moving ordinary objects around the station? What are the marks beside the road? And how long can two survivors stay unnoticed?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
