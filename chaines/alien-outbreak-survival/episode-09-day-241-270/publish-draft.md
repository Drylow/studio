## Title

Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 241 to Day 270

## Description

On day 240, the ship came back, and the alien who once left two people where they stood knelt in front of Reed and Mara and set down three circles instead of two. It isn't asking them to go. It's asking to stay. Now small crafts wait inside the ring every evening, and eighty-two survivors have to choose: up, or home.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 241 to 270 of the invasion. The crowd thins out pair by pair, an alien learns to use a kettle and ring the bell, and someone at the station starts quietly eating half rations. Then the striped alien draws something new in the ledger, and the three of them have to decide who goes and who stays before the ship leaves for good.

Where did the crafts take forty-four people? How long can an alien live on thirty-eight cubes? And who is driving up the road at midnight on day 270?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (every scene), gray school backpack. Counts the Worker's cubes (days 245, 254, 269); speaks to the crowd from the pump island (day 243).
- **Mara**: same stick family, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. Draws the three circles and "Together." (day 241), keeps the "Up / Home / Stayed" columns, draws the "up, or here" choice (day 269); blows out the lantern (day 270).
- **Worker**: plain mint-headed alien, dark suit, flat silver case. Stays at the station from day 241: sits on the curb by the pump, sleeps in the **third corner** of the staff room, nods, uses the kettle, rings the bell on purpose, eats half cubes from day 254, slightly paler around the eyes from day 257 (never gray).
- **Surveyor**: blue shoulder stripe, scanner. Scans the Worker (day 241), comes down alone at noon (day 260) and draws in the ledger, empties its case of cubes into the Worker's (day 269), waves from the door of the last craft (day 270).
- **The schoolhouse pair (from episode 7)**: the woman in the yellow raincoat and the man in the red knitted hat, never named; leave on foot toward the hills on day 248.
- **The boy (about ten) and his family (background)**: the boy writes "WE CALLED OUT / THEY CAME" on the back of his hand; the family goes up on day 251.
- **The crowd (background)**: 82 on day 241, then 66, 34 (day 251), 12 (day 257), 0 (day 266). Pairs walking into the light or away along the road.
- **Night visitors (new, background, day 270)**: only headlights, flashlight beams and footsteps; no faces shown.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: dark straight-edged slab filling the sky over the station (days 241–270), a low hum from day 266; rises on day 270 and becomes a line, a point, nothing; shadows return.
- **Small crafts (new placement)**: from day 243, drop from the ship at dusk and settle inside the ring (at the bend, in the field, on the road past the bicycle), a pale lit circle on the ground under each; people step in and rise with the light.
- **Units**: the giant ring brightens at dusk from day 243; **all go dark one after another on day 270**, including the unit powering the gray box.
- **Three-circle marker (from episode 8)**: on the pavement at the shop door, warm.
- **The Worker's old two-circle marker**: in Reed's pocket.
- **Cubes (new close-up)**: little gray cubes in neat rows in the Worker's case (25 on day 245, 15 and a half on day 254, 38 on day 269 with the Surveyor's 30).
- **Three cars (new, day 270)**: seen only as three pairs of headlights at the bend and on the edge of the forecourt; no make or brand visible.
