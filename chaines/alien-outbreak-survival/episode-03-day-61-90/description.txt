Sixty days after the ship arrived, you finally walk out from under it, and find a red fuel can waiting between the rails. Off the lit track, you find a narrowboat on a quiet canal, apples nobody picked, and a way to ask the aliens a question. Their answer is a map of every place you've slept.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 61 to 90 of the invasion. A test in an open field changes everything they believed about the scanner, the ship starts moving again, and every way out begins to look as if it was left open on purpose. Day 90 brings something the aliens have never done before.

Where do the pairs really go? What was the scanner really looking at on day one? And why is there only one circle this time?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
