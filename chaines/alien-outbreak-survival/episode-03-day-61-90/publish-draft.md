## Title

Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 61 to Day 90

## Description

Sixty days after the ship arrived, you finally walk out from under it, and find a red fuel can waiting between the rails. Off the lit track, you find a narrowboat on a quiet canal, apples nobody picked, and a way to ask the aliens a question. Their answer is a map of every place you've slept.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 61 to 90 of the invasion. A test in an open field changes everything they believed about the scanner, the ship starts moving again, and every way out begins to look as if it was left open on purpose. Day 90 brings something the aliens have never done before.

Where do the pairs really go? What was the scanner really looking at on day one? And why is there only one circle this time?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (worn in every scene, including the day-72 ditch), gray school backpack carried by its handle. Tired and thinner by day 88. Unhurt.
- **Mara**: same stick family as Reed, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. Days 61–63: limps, white tape around one ankle, a plain walking stick on day 63. Tape off from day 75; almost no limp from day 81.
- **Surveyor (striped alien, "the striped figure")**: mint-green round head, black oval eyes, sealed dark suit, blue stripe on the shoulder, gray handheld scanner with a pale beam. Seen on days 72 and 90.
- **Worker (plain alien, "the plain figure")**: same as the Surveyor with no stripe, carrying a flat silver case. Seen on days 69 (alone, drawing in the roll), 72 (kneeling, placing a marker) and 90 (case closed).
- **The waving stranger and companion (new, background, day 75)**: two anonymous stick figures with one bag each, walking along the railway one field away, seen from a distance; one turns and raises an arm. No names, no close-ups.
- **Distant pairs (new, day 85)**: tiny stick silhouettes walking two by two along lit paths into the ring at the foot of the mast, then toward a small craft.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: the dark straight-edged slab. Day 78: a dark wall a quarter of the way up the sky behind the boat. Day 85: hanging over the far end of the valley. Day 90: directly overhead, no shadows on the hilltop.
- **Small craft**: dark, no wheels, no exhaust, silent. Day 78: two pass low over the railway. Day 85: one settles into the ring at the mast and rises toward the ship.
- **Joined-circle markers**: two small circles linked by a short line; glowing along the rails (day 61), on the towpath (day 72), on the lit paths to the mast.
- **Single-circle marker (new, day 90)**: one glowing circle with no line and no partner, beside the narrow gap.
- **Boundary bands**: flat bands of pale light on the ground. Day 82: two parallel bands from the canal edge up the hill (the "hallway"), stopping at the water. Day 90: a full ring around the sheepfold with one narrow gap.
- **Ring of light at the mast (new, day 85)**: a wide flat ring of pale light on the ground around the mast's foot, with lit paths entering from three directions.
- **Red fuel can**: the red jerry can, now with a strip of white tape around its handle and a tiny pencil "61". Day 61 on the rails, day 68 on the towpath, day 69 with the receipt tin on top, days 81–82 on the lock-gate walkway.
- **The Worker's map (new, day 69)**: on the receipt roll, under Reed's drawing (stadium bowl, little figures, big question mark) and Mara's two stick figures: thin perfect lines linking a small square (station) to tiny symbols for the drain, store, bus shelter, hedge, signal hut, stone bridge and boat, ending at a tall thin line with a small triangle on top beside two joined circles.

### Locations

1. **Railway beyond the ship's edge (from episode 2)**: single track in open country, cutting bank, circles glowing along the rails into the distance. Night (glowing) and dawn variants.
2. **Stone bridge (new)**: small stone arch where a country lane crosses a stream; night shelter.
3. **Canal and narrowboat (new)**: flat brown canal, towpath, hedges, the railway one field away; a long narrow green boat tied to two iron rings, small chimney on the roof. Interior: two narrow bunks, small iron wood stove, cupboard, kettle. An old apple tree on the towpath. A ditch in the field beside it (day 72).
4. **Ridge and radio mast (new)**: a tall thin lattice mast on a ridge where the railway disappears; from day 85 seen close enough to show the ring of light at its foot.
5. **Canal lock (new)**: two heavy wooden gates holding the water in a deep stone chamber, a one-plank walkway with a single rail along the top of each gate, a small brick hut beside it; the bands of the "hallway" running uphill from the water's edge.
6. **Hilltop sheepfold (new)**: a round, chest-high dry-stone wall with no roof and one narrow opening, on a bare hilltop above woods and sheep fields; a flat stone in the middle; view over the canal valley to the mast.
