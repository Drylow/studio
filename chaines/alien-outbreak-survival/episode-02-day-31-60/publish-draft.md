## Title

Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 31 to Day 60

## Description

Thirty days after the ship arrived, the aliens finally opened a way out of the gas station, and Reed and Mara take it, just not the way the lights want them to. Off the glowing road, they find shelter in a concrete drain, rainwater at an abandoned hardware store, and a clear view of where every lit road in the city really leads. But every hiding place gets noticed, every boundary closes, and someone else has started writing in their ledger. Day 60 takes them farther than they ever planned, and straight back to something they thought they had left behind.

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf, gray school backpack carried by its handle. From day 57 he looks tired. He is unhurt.
- **Mara**: same stick family as Reed, violet band on one forearm, turquoise backpack. From day 57 she limps, with white tape strapped around one ankle.
- **Striped alien**: smooth pale green round head, black oval eyes, sealed black suit with a high collar, gray shoulder plates, blue stripe on the shoulder, glowing green inverted-triangle badge on the chest, belt and boots. It holds a gray handheld scanner with a pale green-white beam.
- **Plain alien**: same as the striped alien but with no blue stripe, carrying a flat silver metal case. When the case is open, a thin line of pale light trails from it along the ground.
- **Man with suitcase (new, background, day 33)**: same stick family, pulling a wheeled suitcase and walking calmly. Seen from below and at a distance. Give him one plain jacket color that differs from Reed's and Mara's.
- **Distant walkers (new, day 42)**: tiny stick silhouettes with bags, in pairs and alone, each with a short trail of lit circles ahead of it.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: an enormous dark slab with perfectly straight edges filling the top of the sky over the city. On day 60 it sits behind the characters as a dark wall on the horizon, with open sky above them.
- **Small craft (new design)**: a smaller dark craft with no wheels or exhaust, sitting just above the road or settling into the stadium.
- **Dark units (new design)**: upright smooth dark machines taller than a person, with no face or limbs and a glowing joined-circle symbol near the base. They glide silently just above the ground or rails.
- **Joined-circle marker**: two small circles linked by a short line. It glows pale when lit and appears on the road, at unit bases, on the rails and as a small drawing at the end of the receipt roll.
- **Measurement mark**: a vertical line with a short stroke across it, scratched on the fence post on day 40, the same mark as under the station shelf in episode 1.
- **Boundary band**: a narrow band of pale light lying flat on the ground. It closes around the gas station on day 31 and around the hardware store on day 48.
- **Red fuel can**: the red jerry can from episode 1, shown empty and rocking slightly between the rails on day 60.

### Locations

Five new places, plus the station at the start.

1. **Gas station (from episode 1, day 31 only)**: cracked shop window, the red-and-gray canopy, pumps, the red can and the circle of pale light that opens one gap at the roadside path and then closes. It has had no power since day 7, so there are no lit displays.
2. **The road beyond the bend (new view)**: a two-lane road with stopped cars, a grassy bank and a field fence. Dark units stand at intervals, and their circles light one by one ahead of anyone walking on the road.
3. **Concrete drain under the road (new)**: a round concrete tunnel with a thin trickle of water down the middle and two dry ledges. The mouth opens onto the bank near the fence line.
4. **Hardware store with fenced garden yard (new)**: a low flat-roofed building with a half-open metal shutter and half-empty aisles. At the back is a storeroom with a workbench, pallets and a back door. Outside, the garden yard has a fence, and the tarp is tied between two posts above the buckets.
5. **Highway footbridge (new)**: a pedestrian bridge with railings over a wide highway lined with units. From it, lit roads converge across the city toward a glowing stadium bowl under the mothership, with dark floodlights.
6. **Railway line and signal hut (new)**: a single track sunk below a row of backyards, with wooden ties and gray stones. At a junction stands a small brick signal hut with a rain barrel under its gutter. The day-60 variant is the same track in open late light, with the ship's edge behind, the red can between the rails and lit markers running toward open countryside.
