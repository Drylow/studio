Thirty days after the ship arrived, a gap opens in the light around the gas station. You take it, just not the way the lights want you to. Off the glowing road, you find shelter in a concrete drain, rainwater at an abandoned hardware store, and a clear view of where every lit road in the city really leads.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 31 to 60 of the invasion. Every hiding place gets noticed, every boundary closes, and someone else has started writing in their ledger. Day 60 takes them farther than they ever planned, and straight back to something they thought they had left behind.

Where do the lit roads really lead? Why does the scanner always stop on what you carry? And who expected you to be standing exactly where you are?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
