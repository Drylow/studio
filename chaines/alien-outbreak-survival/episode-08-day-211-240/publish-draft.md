## Title

Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 211 to Day 240

## Description

Two hundred and ten days after the ship arrived, Reed and Mara walk back into the gas station where it all began, and every light is on. The milk is still by the pump, the bicycle is still one step from the first marker, and something has been quietly powering the place for them. Then the little entrance bell starts ringing, and the people who are left begin to arrive.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 211 to 240 of the invasion. The ledger is read out loud to a crowd, a new star with straight edges grows in the sky, and hundreds of units glide in to surround the station. On day 240, Reed finally gets to finish what he was doing on day one.

Who powered the station? What does the ring mean this time? And what is the alien trying to say with three circles?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (every scene), gray school backpack. Day 240: alone at the lit payment machine with a crumpled bill.
- **Mara**: same stick family, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. Hangs the bell (day 216), writes "Together." (day 228) and the boxed "Day 240" with two small joined circles (day 237).
- **The schoolhouse pair (from episode 7, day 219)**: the woman in the yellow raincoat and the man in the red knitted hat, never named; they bring eggs wrapped in a scarf. (The older couple stayed home and is not shown.)
- **The crowd (new, background)**: survivors of the same stick family arriving in pairs from day 216 (two, then more; thirty-four on day 222; eighty-two from day 225): mostly pairs, a few families, a boy of about ten, one baby. Bags, blankets, people sleeping in the shop, under the canopy and in cars.
- **Surveyor and Worker (day 240 only)**: step out of a small craft on the road, exactly as on day one; the Worker carries its flat silver case again, kneels and sets down a **three-circle marker**.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: day 231 a bright star low over the city that doesn't twinkle; then bigger each night, with straight edges; day 237 as big as a hand at arm's length; day 239 a quarter of the sky; **day 240 slides over the station, every shadow disappears**.
- **Small craft**: settles on the road on day 240, no wheels, no smoke.
- **Units**: one stands behind the shop beside the gray power box, a thin pale line of light from its circles into the box (it powers the station). **Day 234: hundreds glide in silently a hand's width above the ground and stand in a giant ring** around the station, the road, the bend and the field.
- **The first marker (from episode 1)**: two joined circles glowing on the pavement by the shop door, the bicycle lying one step from it.
- **The Worker's old marker**: in the tin, wrapped in cloth; **glows again from midnight on day 239**, warm.
- **Three-circle marker (new, day 240)**: three circles in a row joined by two short lines, glowing.
