On day 151, the gap in the ice is only wide enough for one, and this time it isn't meant for Reed or Mara. The alien hiding with them refuses to walk through it, and its last piece of food runs out. Outside, its striped partner waits in the snow, day and night, and the ship is counting down to day 180.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 151 to 180 of the invasion. They burn the mill to stay warm, fish through a hole in the ice, watch the last pairs walk across the frozen river, and discover what the light really can't hold. With one match left and the ship directly overhead, three of them lean on a frozen iron wheel.

What happens to whatever is inside a ring on day 180? Can moving water break the light? And what did the alien draw at the very end of the ledger?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
