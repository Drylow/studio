Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 151 to Day 180

## Description

On day 151, the gap in the ice is only wide enough for one, and this time it isn't meant for Reed or Mara. The alien hiding with them refuses to walk through it, and its last piece of food runs out. Outside, its striped partner waits in the snow, day and night, and the ship is counting down to day 180.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 151 to 180 of the invasion. They burn the mill to stay warm, fish through a hole in the ice, watch the last pairs walk across the frozen river, and discover what the light really can't hold. With one match left and the ship directly overhead, three of them lean on a frozen iron wheel.

What happens to whatever is inside a ring on day 180? Can moving water break the light? And what did the alien draw at the very end of the ledger?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (every scene), gray school backpack. Hungry; knuckles cracked from the gate work (day 172); unhurt otherwise.
- **Mara**: same stick family, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. Calls out to the Surveyor (day 160); waves from the boat (day 180).
- **Worker (plain alien)**: no case until day 180; brown wool blanket; **face gray "like ash"** from day 160 (lying on its side by the fire); eats nothing after day 152 except the half cubes the Surveyor gives it. Day 180: leans on the gate wheel, takes back its flat silver case, kneels and sets a joined-circle marker beside the boat, waves, boards the last craft with the Surveyor.
- **Surveyor (striped alien)**: blue shoulder stripe, scanner; stands outside the gap on the ice day and night with the silver case at its feet, snow on its shoulders. From day 160 comes into the mill every evening, kneels by the Worker, breaks a pale cube in half and gives half away; grayer and slower. Day 180: hands the case back, turns away from the marker, walks away side by side with the Worker.
- **Last pairs (background, days 166–178)**: dozens of tiny stick figures in pairs walking across the frozen river along glowing joined circles to a huge ring at the bend; some carry bags, some hold hands.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: day 163 its straight edge creeps across the sky toward the river; day 166 over the bend; **day 178 directly over the island (no shadows)**; **day 180 rises and disappears**: every shadow comes back at once; plain blue winter sky.
- **Small craft**: many, landing in the big ring at the bend (fourteen counted on day 166); the last one takes the two aliens on day 180.
- **Ring on the ice**: around the island, narrow gap and single circle, open day and night; glows brighter on day 178; **day 180 breaks where the water shows and fades in pieces**.
- **Kettle test (day 169)**: boiling water poured on the band melts a puddle; the band flickers out over the puddle for about a minute, then returns when it refreezes.
- **Joined-circle marker**: set on the ice beside the boat by the Worker on day 180.
- **The Worker's drawings**: the stadium ring and the island ring with crafts and arrows up, the boxed "180", itself walking out with the gap closing on two figures, then crossed out (day 154); two joined circles under Reed's boat and two under the aliens, one line beneath both pairs (day 175); **at the very end of the roll: the ship going up, sixty short lines, an arrow pointing down** (found day 180).
