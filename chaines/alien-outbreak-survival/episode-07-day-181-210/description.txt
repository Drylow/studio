For the first time in 180 days, there's nothing in the sky but sky. Reed and Mara drift into a village where every door is marked and every house is empty, with one match left and winter all around them. Then Mara finds something they missed at the tip of the alien's last drawing: the ship is coming back, and it's coming back to the gas station where it all began.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 181 to 210 of the invasion as they walk their whole story backward: the mill, the hilltop sheepfold, the lock where the lantern sank, the narrowboat, the railway. They meet people who hid through all of it, and one evening every unit in the country lights up at once. When the lights start leading them home, they follow them for the first time.

Who is still down here? What woke the units up? And why is every light at the gas station switched on?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
