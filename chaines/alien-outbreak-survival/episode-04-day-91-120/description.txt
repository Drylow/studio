Ninety days after the ship arrived, the aliens open a gap that's only wide enough for one. Reed and Mara refuse to choose, and for the first time in the whole invasion, someone touches the light. What it does to them sends them into an icy stream, down a river at night, and past a town where the pairs are still being collected.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 91 to 120 of the invasion. The scanner finds them and leaves them where they are, the red fuel can comes back by river with a second number on it, and winter is coming fast. Day 120 brings a visitor they never expected to see alone.

What happens when you step on the light? Who signed the can? And what did it cost the alien who wiped away their footprints?

An original animated science-fiction story from One Stick Left.
