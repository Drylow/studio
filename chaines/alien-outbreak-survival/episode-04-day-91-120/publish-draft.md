Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 91 to Day 120

## Description

Ninety days after the ship arrived, the aliens open a gap that's only wide enough for one. Reed and Mara refuse to choose, and for the first time in the whole invasion, someone touches the light. What it does to them sends them into an icy stream, down a river at night, and past a town where the pairs are still being collected.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 91 to 120 of the invasion. The scanner finds them and leaves them where they are, the red fuel can comes back by river with a second number on it, and winter is coming fast. Day 120 brings a visitor they never expected to see alone.

What happens when you step on the light? Who signed the can? And what did it cost the alien who wiped away their footprints?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (every scene), gray school backpack. Thin, cold and wet from day 95; unhurt.
- **Mara**: same stick family, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. No limp. She rows the boat, digs potatoes with her hands, catches the fish (day 112).
- **Surveyor (striped alien, "the striped figure")**: mint-green round head, black oval eyes, sealed dark suit, blue stripe on the shoulder, gray handheld scanner. Seen only on day 91, outside the sheepfold band, scanner lowered.
- **Worker (plain alien, "the plain figure")**: same with no stripe. Days 91 and 94 with its flat silver case (day 94 at night: kneels and wipes away the glowing footprints one by one with its gloved hand). **Day 120: NO case, empty hands, frost on its shoulders, suit soaked dark up to the chest**, sitting at the island end of the weir walkway, then knocking on the mill door.
- **Waiting pairs (background, day 106)**: tiny stick figures standing two by two inside a ring of light in a riverside park, then walking to a small craft.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: the dark straight-edged slab. Days 91–95: directly over the hilltop (flat gray light, no shadows). From day 97: far behind, still over the hill and the mast. Day 120: low on the far horizon.
- **Small craft**: dark, no wheels, silent. Day 106: one settles in the riverside park and lifts; a second drifts over the river sweeping its pale scanner line across the water and the rowing boat.
- **Units**: smooth dark upright machines with joined circles glowing at their base, along the riverside road of the town (day 106).
- **Single-circle marker**: glowing beside the gap on day 91 until dusk, then fading out.
- **Boundary band**: ring of pale light around the sheepfold; the gap closes at dusk on day 91.
- **Glowing footprints (new, days 94–95)**: pale footprints, same light as the band, left on the grass by anyone who has stepped on the band; the soles of the shoes glow faintly in the dark; the glow fades in water.
- **Red fuel can**: red jerry can with the old tape strip and tiny pencil "61" on the handle, **plus a second strip of tape below it with "91" in thin perfect lines** (day 109). Floats down the river and bumps the boat; from day 109 tied to the stern of the rowing boat by fishing line.
