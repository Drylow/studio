Alien Outbreak Survival: Day 121 to Day 150

## Description

On day 121, one of the aliens knocks on the door of the island mill, soaked, alone, and without the silver case it has carried since day one. Reed and Mara let it in. With nothing but their pencil and the receipt roll, it finally answers the question they asked months ago: where the pairs go, and why the circle on the hilltop was alone.

Follow Reed and Mara through days 121 to 150 of the invasion. Snow covers the river, strange footprints walk the far bank, the alien's own food starts running out, and a date appears at the top of the ledger in a box. Then the river freezes, and the one rule that kept them safe stops working.

Where does the ship take the pairs? What was the last seat? And who is the gap for this time?

### Characters

- **Reed (you)**: round white head, black dot eyes, thin black stick body, white mitten hands and round white feet, blue-gray scarf (every scene), gray school backpack. Thin and cold; unhurt.
- **Mara**: same stick family, purple band on the right forearm, teal backpack. No limp.
- **Worker (plain alien, "the plain figure")**: mint-green round head, black oval eyes, sealed dark suit, no stripe. **No case all episode.** Day 121: soaked, frost on its shoulders, draws in the flour dust with a finger. From day 124 it draws with Reed's pencil, holding it clumsily. Sits on an empty flour sack by the millstones; from day 139 by the upstairs hearth, wrapped in a brown wool blanket. **Fading (new state)**: from day 139 slower, its pale green face a shade grayer. Carries small pale cubes (its food) in a suit pocket.
- **Surveyor (striped alien, "the striped figure")**: mint-green head, blue shoulder stripe, scanner. Day 142: on the far bank in the snow, scanner in one hand, **the Worker's flat silver case in the other**. Day 150: on the ice outside the ring, case at its feet, scanner lowered.

### Creatures, machines and signs

- **Mothership**: dark straight-edged slab, far on the horizon all episode.
- **Units**: smooth dark upright machines, joined circles at the base, along a village main street; they light pair by pair when someone steps on the road (day 136).
- **Door marks (new, day 136)**: small joined-circle marks at hand height on almost every front door of the village.
- **Boundary bands**: day 142, one band along each river bank at the water's edge; days 145–148, the bands sit on the edge ice and follow it as it grows; day 150, a full ring on the frozen river around the island with one narrow gap and a single glowing circle.
- **Alien footprints in snow (new)**: long, narrow prints going back and forth along the far bank (day 127, day 142).
- **Pale food cubes (new)**: small pale cubes the size of a sugar lump, kept in a pocket of the Worker's suit (5 on day 122, then 4, 2, 1).
- **The Worker's drawings (new)**: in flour dust, its case and the same case crossed out (day 121); on the receipt roll, the hill with footprints, itself kneeling, the Surveyor holding the case, itself in water up to the chest (day 124); the stadium, the mast ring, the park, pairs, a craft, the ship full of rows of joined circles, an arrow curving off the top of the page (day 130); the ship full except one single circle, forty-seven short lines, an arrow up, and the mill with two figures and a third with no case (day 133); two joined circles, one with a stripe (day 142); a circle around the mill, smaller, smaller (day 145).
