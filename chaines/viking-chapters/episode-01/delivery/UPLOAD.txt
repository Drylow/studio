TITLE
Why Being a Viking Was Worse Than You Think

DESCRIPTION — COPY THIS BLOCK
A ship. A sword. A fortune. That was the plan.

In reality, your fence is broken, the sheep is escaping, and the sea is the easy part of the story.

Follow Eirik, an imagined Norse farmer around AD 950, through farm work, a dangerous voyage, uncertain rewards, trade, winter preparations, and a dispute that a sword cannot solve. Discover the ordinary lives behind the Viking legend.

This is an original illustrated historical scenario, informed by archaeology and museum research. Eirik and his household are fictional; their experiences illustrate the period rather than the biography of a real person. Norse communities and individual circumstances varied.
