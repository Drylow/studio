A fast ship, a chest of silver, your name in a song: that was the plan. In reality, you sleep on a row of oars, eat cold porridge, bail seawater in the dark, and your share of the loot gets chopped into pieces and weighed. Follow Eirik, an imagined Norse farmer of the tenth century, on a raiding voyage with Bjorn's crew: the crossing, the raid and the people it harmed, the wounds, the sharing of the silver and the long trip home. This original illustrated scenario is built from museum research, archaeology and sources such as the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle; Eirik and his crew are fictional.
