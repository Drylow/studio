## YouTube description

A painted shield, a sword, braids flying into battle: that is the legend. In reality, you run the farm while your husband is away raiding, spin and weave the cloth behind sails that took years of work, marry by family bargain, and face childbirth with no doctor coming. Follow Aslaug, an imagined Norse woman of the tenth century, through a year on a farm in western Norway: the keys and the law, marriage, bride-price and divorce, the loom, the winter stores, the enslaved people beside her, childbirth, and the women who traded, travelled and raised runestones. Then we open Birka grave Bj 581 and ask what scholars actually conclude about warrior women. This original illustrated scenario is built from museum research, archaeology, peer-reviewed studies, Norse laws, sagas and runic inscriptions; Aslaug and her household are fictional. Subscribe to Viking Chapters for another life behind the legends.
