## YouTube description

Wooden swords, tiny shields and a childhood of adventure: that is the legend. In reality, you had to be accepted at birth to be raised at all, you were put to work as soon as you could carry a bucket, your toys were training for adult chores, and childhood was over at sixteen, or at your wedding. Follow Thora, the imagined daughter of Eirik and Aslaug, through her childhood on a farm in tenth-century Norway: the father's knee and the water-sprinkling, the exposure of unwanted newborns, chores and spindles, toy boats, bone skates and board games, fostering, learning without a school, and growing up far too early. Then we look at a tiny stone animal from a Viking Age farm in Iceland that archaeologists still cannot identify. This original illustrated scenario is built from museum research, archaeology, Norse poetry, sagas and laws; Thora and her family are fictional. Subscribe to Viking Chapters for another life behind the legends.
