# Aztec 01 Research Review

Period: Mexico-Tenochtitlan and Tlatelolco around 1500, before Spanish invasion.
Illustrative produce-growing household, not an identified historical diary.

- [Templo Mayor Museum, agriculture](https://www.templomayor.inah.gob.mx/salas-del-museo/sala-7-agricultura): agricultural and urban infrastructure, canals and canoe traffic.
- [INAH, chinampa archaeology and crops](https://www.inah.gob.mx/boletines/el-sistema-chinampero-de-la-cuenca-de-mexico-en-la-nueva-edicion-de-arqueologia-mexicana): cultivation and archaeological dating, not drifting rafts.
- [UNAM, Alejandra Davila Montoya, Tlatelolco market](https://www.noticonquista.unam.mx/amoxtli/1890/1890): material and social variety, types of exchange, household purchases and organized trade.
- [INAH, tianquiztli supervision](https://www.inah.gob.mx/index.php?Itemid=329&catid=35&id=1684%3Aesclarecen-enigma&option=com_content&view=article): measures and dispute oversight. No invented exact penalty.
- [Getty Research Institute, Florentine Codex](https://florentinecodex.getty.edu/book/10/folio/vir): occupational categories. Colonial-period compilation from Indigenous knowledge; not a literal recording of 1500.
- [INAH, archaeological excavation of city canals](https://arqueologia.inah.gob.mx/publico/especifico.php?id=NDc%3D): domestic vessels and production evidence from preconquest urban context.

## Boundaries

No horses, European colonial arches, iron household knives, copper coin price
labels, floating raft chinampas, universal cacao breakfasts, exact invented
tribute schedules, or daily spectacle of human sacrifice. No simultaneous
claim that everyone was free, prosperous, or a professional long-distance trader.
Trade events, purchases, personal relationships and route are illustrative.
Covered adult cartoon garments are a deliberate style adaptation, not a claim
that every Mexica person wore long trousers and sleeves.
