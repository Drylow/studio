Your master puts a lightsaber into a stranger's hands and tells her to take your place.

Neris stands across the stone chamber, turning the hilt over in her hand. You recognise the weapon. You repaired its switch for Lord Varek, who is now sitting beside the only door. You thought learning his secrets would make you harder to replace. But he has brought someone here to see whether you deserve to keep them. Before she ignites the blade, you notice the repair you left beneath its grip.

The switch is the same sort you once had to fix with a loading ramp hanging above you.

Back then, your name is Soren, and you work in a repair dock where nobody waits for a mechanic to finish eating. A freighter needs its ramp before the next berth opens. You are underneath it, holding a loose connector while the pilot shouts from the cockpit. You ask her to keep the controls still. She reaches for them anyway, and the ramp begins coming down.

Your hand goes up before you can move. The ramp stops with its weight hanging above your shoulder. You stare at the gap between your palm and the metal. Nothing is touching. The pilot cuts the power, and you scramble out with the connector still in your other hand. You tell yourself the emergency brake must have caught. Then you notice the brake assembly sitting on your bench.

Lysa, the courier waiting for that freighter, has seen everything. She asks how you held it. You point at the disconnected controls. She looks at them, then asks again. Before you answer, a passenger in black robes walks down the dock. He was close enough to see your raised hand. His name is Varek, and he asks you to bring the connector with you.

He takes you into an empty cargo hold and closes the hatch. A loose metal clip rests on a crate between you. He asks you to lift it without touching it. You spend several uncomfortable minutes staring at a piece of scrap. When he slides the crate toward the edge of a loading platform, you reach for the clip. It jumps against your palm before your fingers close.

Varek asks what you felt beneath the ramp. You tell him you thought it was going to crush you. He places the clip on the crate again. This time, you know something is possible. You can move it a short distance when you remember the ramp falling. When your attention slips, the clip drops. Varek picks it up and tells you he can teach you to do more.

You ask what the teaching costs. He wants an apprentice who will travel with him, follow instructions and keep what he learns to himself. You have no place on his ship unless you take the offer. Lysa catches you putting your tools into a small case. She asks whether you even know where you're going. You tell her that you will finally be able to choose something beyond the next broken ramp.

Varek gives you a charcoal tunic and a maroon sash. Your tools stay in their case beneath the bunk. His ship carries you to a remote stone residence above a dry valley. The training room is almost empty: a rack, a floor marked by old impacts and a heavy door that closes slowly enough to make you watch it. You ask whether other students are coming. Varek says that he already has an apprentice.

He follows the Sith tradition of one master and one apprentice. Darth Bane established that rule to end the fighting that had weakened the Sith. Varek describes the apprentice's purpose while he walks around you. You will learn what he knows, become strong enough to challenge him and eventually take his place. He says this without looking worried. You are still trying to lift a clip.

The first lessons leave your hands shaking. You raise objects, hold them steady and lose them whenever Varek interrupts you. He asks questions while a stone block is suspended above the floor. If you answer too quickly, the block drops. If you keep staring at it, he knocks your feet out from beneath you. You have to recover your balance while keeping the thing in the air.

You improve when you stop waiting for each interruption. You begin listening to his movement as well as watching the stone. On one attempt, you turn before his foot reaches you. The block remains suspended. Varek leaves it there while he opens the door. You cannot follow until he lets you put it down. Even a successful lesson ends when he decides it does.

He teaches you to fight with a training weapon before giving you a real blade. The first time you rush him, he catches your wrist and sends the weapon across the room. You fetch it. He tells you to use the frustration you are already feeling. On the next exchange, you push harder. He steps aside and makes you chase him into the same mistake.

Eventually you make him change position. A loose floor plate shifts beneath his boot when you reach through the Force. He stops your next strike, but you have made him move. You expect him to mention the plate. Instead, he asks you to repair the door mechanism after training. It has been catching at the end of its travel. You spend the evening with the housing open, learning how much stone the motor is supposed to move.

Your first mission takes you back among cargo ships. Varek wants a sealed navigation archive delivered to him. Its current holder is preparing to leave through a busy transfer port. He sends you to arrange transport before the shipment moves. You expect him to come with you. He gives you a transmitter and tells you to report when you have the archive.

Lysa is working at the port. She recognises the tool case you still carry and asks whether your new employer has broken something already. You tell her that you need a pilot who can make a difficult collection. She asks for the destination and payment before agreeing. You give her both. She points at your robes and asks whether the passenger will be as quiet as the cargo.

You board her small courier ship with the instructions Varek supplied. Lysa can get you near the transfer vessel, but its crew refuses to hand over the archive. You send Varek a message. He tells you that an apprentice should not need his master to settle a disagreement with a cargo crew. You return to the loading hatch and demand that they open it.

A guard raises his weapon from inside. You push him back through the Force. His shot strikes the hatch frame as he falls, and the locking mechanism jams. The archive is still behind the door. Lysa pulls you away from the opening before a second guard reaches it. She asks whether that made the collection easier. You have to cut the damaged housing open with a tool you brought from the dock.

While you work, she keeps the corridor clear. You can move a person across a room, but you still need somebody beside you while your hands are inside a broken machine. The hatch opens far enough to recover the crate. You leave with it while Lysa watches the guards. On her ship, she makes you pay for the damage to the ramp where you dragged the load aboard.

Varek is pleased that the archive reached him. He barely asks about the crew. You tell him that the hatch jammed. He says you should have forced it sooner. Later, when you try opening the archive yourself, its locks refuse your access. Varek takes it from you and carries it into a private chamber. You have delivered a secret that you are still forbidden to read.

He gives you the parts for a lightsaber after that mission. You assemble the hilt at your old tool case, testing each connection until the blade ignites red above the bench. You shut it off immediately because the ceiling is closer than you expected. Varek watches from the doorway. He asks whether you plan to keep staring at it or learn to use it.

The new blade changes your training. A mistake can cut through the rack instead of merely knocking it over. You have to control where you strike. Varek drives you toward a wall, then stops with his weapon near your shoulder. He asks what you will do when an opponent is stronger. You try to push him away. His boots barely move. He tells you to find an answer before the next lesson.

You begin looking at the room differently. The door, the floor plates and the shelves are things you understand. Varek knows exactly how you swing your blade. He pays less attention when you are repairing a hinge. You keep practising after he leaves, using small movements rather than trying to lift the heaviest object. The tool clip travels beneath the bench where he cannot see it from the entrance.

Varek notices your progress on a later mission. You recover a memory core from a ruined archive without bringing the ceiling down around it. Lysa flies you there because she trusts your payment more than his promises. You move the fallen shelf just enough for her to reach the case. When she gets back, you lower it onto its supports. You both leave before the damaged corridor gives way.

The core contains a record Varek expected to keep private. You see his ship's route before you seal it inside the case. There is another landing site in the valley below his residence. Someone has been arriving while you are away. You copy the arrival times onto your own transmitter. When Varek asks whether you examined the core, you say you checked that it was intact.

He studies you for longer than usual. That night, the private chamber is locked even after he leaves it. You return to the training room and find your practice clip on the floor by the entrance. You had left it beneath the bench. Varek has moved it where you will notice. You cannot tell how much else he has found, so you begin checking your tool case before every lesson.

Lysa hears you ask about the lower landing site during your next flight. She knows the route. A young woman named Neris has been using it. You ask whether Neris travels with a weapon. Lysa tells you that she travels with an instructor's invitation. You stop asking questions for a while. Then you ask Lysa to keep her ship ready near the residence when you return.

She asks whether you intend to leave. You tell her that you need to know what Varek is doing. She says those are different journeys and she charges for either. You give her the remaining payment from your last mission. Before landing, she makes you look at the exit ramp. If she opens it for you, she expects you to come aboard instead of starting another fight in front of it.

Inside, Varek tells you that a visitor will join the next lesson. He asks you to repair the switch on a spare lightsaber. The hilt has a worn connector beneath its grip, much like the one from your old cargo ramp. You replace the faulty part and test it. Then you leave a small retaining clip exposed beneath the casing. The blade works, but the connector can move if that clip is pulled.

You place the weapon where Varek asked. He does not inspect the repair. He has watched you keep his doors and weapons working for so long that he accepts the hilt when you hand it over. You return your tools to their case. The following morning, Neris walks into the stone chamber wearing a navy tunic and a violet sash. Varek gives her the repaired weapon.

Now you hear him tell her to take your place. She looks at you before she ignites the blade. Varek explains that you have begun hiding your progress, and an apprentice who hides things has already started challenging his master. Neris asks what happens if she loses. He tells her that she should concentrate on winning. You can hear the door motor starting behind his chair.

She attacks while the door is closing. You meet the first strike and turn her blade away from your face. She has been trained well enough to recover before you can push her back. Varek watches you trade blows across the chamber. You keep your weapon between you and Neris while the small clip beneath her hilt starts sliding from its housing.

When you pull it free through the Force, her blade shuts off. She stares at the empty hilt. You could strike before she moves, but you turn toward Varek. He rises from his chair with his own weapon already active. Neris has made you cross the room and show him your first trick. He sends the fallen clip against the wall as you reach for it again.

You retreat toward the heavy door. Varek follows, cutting across your guard until your shoulder hits the stone frame. You cannot hold him back for long. His next strike knocks your blade low, and he reaches toward your throat. You pull the door's exposed catch rather than trying to move his whole body. The slab drives into his weapon arm before he can turn it aside.

The motor strains against him. You know where its housing opens because you spent an evening repairing it. You force the latch fully down. Varek has to release his grip on you to push the door away, and you step clear while his attention is on the stone. Neris is behind his chair, watching. She leaves her broken weapon on the floor.

Varek gets the door moving again and comes toward you. This time, you have space to meet him. You catch his blade near the frame, turn with it and strike before he can restore his stance. His weapon falls against the threshold. When he stops reaching for it, you shut yours off. Neris keeps watching from the other side of the room. You can hear a ship landing outside.

Lysa opens the ramp as promised. You walk toward it with your tool case. She asks whether you are leaving. Behind you, Neris asks what she should do now. You stop beside the ramp and look back at the residence. Varek's archive is still inside. His ship, his lessons and the records he kept from you are all here. You put the tool case down on the ground.

Lysa waits until you tell her you are staying. She returns the unused part of your payment and closes the ramp. You ask whether she will still take work from you. She says she will collect ordinary cargo from the lower landing site. She will not return to this chamber to collect another person you have decided to test. You watch her ship lift from the valley before going back inside.

Neris has picked up the damaged hilt. You ask why she left it on the floor during the fight. She tells you that Varek would have killed either of you if it suited him. You show her the loose connector and let her put the clip back. The weapon ignites. You take a step away before telling her to switch it off. She has noticed that step.

You become the Sith Lord who keeps the residence and takes Neris as an apprentice. The locked archive opens to the access device you recovered from Varek. You read the records you once carried here without permission to touch them. Some concern the places where you worked. Others list candidates he visited while you were training. You move that list into a drawer before Neris enters.

Her lessons begin in the room you know. You let her lift the stone block while you walk behind it. She turns before your foot reaches her, and you recognise the movement. Later, the door catches at the end of its travel. Neris offers to repair the housing. You tell her to leave it until you have checked it yourself. She looks at the worn latch, then puts her tools away.

That evening, she leaves the small retaining clip on your desk. It came loose while she was checking her lightsaber. You look from the piece of metal to the apprentice waiting by the door. Your hand settles over your own hilt before you notice that hers are empty. Neris is still waiting for you to tell her whether she should finish the repair.
