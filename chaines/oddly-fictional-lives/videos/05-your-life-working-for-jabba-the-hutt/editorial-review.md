## Research brief and canon boundary

authority, obligations and danger, and whether you can actually collect your pay
and leave. Original adult worker Tavi, fellow worker Nima, clerk Venn, hunter Kade
and merchant Orr are authored fiction. Their accounts, collection jobs, missing
power-cell shipment, fraud and departure are NOT claimed as recorded Star Wars
events or the official organisation chart of Jabba's staff.

Verified canon anchors, consulted directly:

- [Star Wars Databank: Jabba](https://www.starwars.com/databank/jabba-the-hutt):
  Hutt crime lord, palace, debts, hired bounty hunters and his rancor pit.
- [Star Wars Databank: Rancor](https://www.starwars.com/databank/rancor):
  a creature kept below the throne room; distinctive long-arm anatomy.
- [Star Wars Databank: Mos Eisley](https://www.starwars.com/databank/mos-eisley-spaceport):
  Tatooine's spaceport and its mix of servants, smugglers and pilots.

The palace setting and danger are anchored to those sources; the plot is original
and takes place while Jabba and his rancor are alive, before the film's rescue.
