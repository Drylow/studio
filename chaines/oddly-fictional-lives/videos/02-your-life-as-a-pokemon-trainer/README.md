# Your Life as a Pokemon Trainer

Episode 02 for Oddly Fictional Lives, chosen 12 October, pilot finished 13 October
2026 (Paris). The God of War episode is already published according to the user.

**[Open the Pokemon project in TubeForge](https://tools.kanye.studio/2d#/video/your-life-as-a-pokemon-trainer-df03b3/images)**
Use the existing tools.kanye.studio account. No separate TubeForge login.

- `script.txt`: clean English narration, 3,076 words, approximately 15:40 at the
  previous voiceover's measured pace. Estimated only; no new voice yet.
- `structure.json`: chapters and paragraph boundaries, no invented timecodes.
- `research.md`: actual sources, reference-register evidence and fiction scope.
- `editorial-review.md` / `verification.json`: editorial and technical checks.
- `visual-direction.txt` / `visual-bible.json`: style and current reference files.
- `production.json`: live project, pilot media checks and actual production state.

Visual assets and exact calls: `output/imagegen/oddly-fictional-pokemon-02/`.
`A-REGARDER/01.png` through `03.png` are the current 1920x1080 images. Source
generation was 1376x768, then native Flow upscale to 2752x1536, then an explicit
crop from the same camera and downscale to 1080p. This is not native 1080p
generation. Original wides, low-resolution sources and rejected identity drafts
are retained as archives, not selected character references.

Nico: red cap, blue zip jacket, charcoal trousers, brown boots, round flat white
head and hands. No backpack. Charmander: recognizable orange creature, one tail
and flame. Center nurse: white round head, mint uniform and striped white cap.
Only these three current identities are installed and reviewed in the shared
Pokemon style. Others/evolved forms still need separate references.
