# Your Life as a Pokemon Trainer — production brief

Chosen by the user on 12 October 2026. Channel: Oddly Fictional Lives.
Target: 15–16 minutes. Original English second-person fictional journey in Kanto.
This is an original trainer story inspired by the classic Kanto games, not an
official character biography, exact game walkthrough, anime tournament recap,
or claim that these invented matches occur in canon.

## Five-field Faceless OS brief

TOPIC: A first-time trainer with Charmander works toward the Kanto Championship.
ANGLE: Hidden cost of the familiar badge journey: caring for the partners you
keep asking to win. Concrete mistakes, training, rest, rival assistance and a
team whose evolution changes the daily work. The rival becomes an ally before
becoming the final opponent. No invented universal hierarchy of trainer ranks.
PROOF BANK: Official Kanto overview establishes Pallet Town/Oak, Brock's Rock
Gym, Misty's Water Gym, Gym Badges, Team Rocket and the Elite Four with Lorelei,
Bruno, Agatha and Lance. Official postgame article explicitly describes all
eight Gyms and becoming League Champion. Official Pokedex establishes the
Charmander–Charmeleon–Charizard line and Charizard's Fire/Flying types.
HOOK DIRECTION: Carry the injured starter into the Center after a first loss;
resolve the immediate health question before flashing back to choosing it.
STRUCTURE HINT: A single lived journey, relational rival arc, three named team
members and two significant failed challenges. Chapters are events, not ranks.

and training sessions, trades declined, intervention against a Rocket grunt,
Sabrina rematch, failed Lorelei run, Milo reaching Champion first, winning
match and return to the Center are authored fiction. No invented events are
presented as episodes from an official game or anime. No fabricated statistics.
Narration stays immersive; this distinction belongs in description/production
notes rather than making the narrator repeatedly apologize for the fiction.
