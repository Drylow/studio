# The Wall — authored research and full script review

Second authorised Oddly Fictional Lives production, after One Piece. No new
publication date is inferred from the old uploads or the nine workplace remakes.

TOPIC: Your Life on the Wall (Game of Thrones).
ANGLE: A newcomer who nearly opens the gate to a dead man learns to distinguish
real danger from frightened living people. He becomes useful through observation,
work and relationships rather than being promoted into every famous office.
PROOF BANK:
- [Night's Watch](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Night%27s_Watch), secondary
  reference with book chapter sources: stewards, builders and rangers are three
  orders under the Lord Commander, not three sequential ranks. Men in every order
  may need to fight. Edren's later transfer is an authored individual decision.
- The same Watch reference supports lifelong service and the restrictions on
  marriage, family and inheritance. The script paraphrases rather than quoting
  the oath. It does not claim a compulsory criminal origin for every recruit.
- [Builders](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Builders): maintenance of the
  Wall and its castles. It supports Torr's role without inventing military ranks.
- [Lord Snow](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/index.php/Lord_Snow): the television
  Castle Black yard, barracks, mess hall and Wall lift. No precise Wall height,
  structural load or ice-engineering calculation is asserted.
- [White Walkers](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/White_Walkers): these are
  distinct from reanimated wights; they can break ordinary steel and resist fire.
  [Wight](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Wight) supports the blue eyes and
  burning dead. Search-indexed excerpts were accessible; direct fetch restricted.
- [Beyond the Wall](https://gameofthrones.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_the_Wall_%28episode%29)
  documents the show's distinction between a Walker and its following dead.
  Obsidian killing a Walker is retained as a setting rule, never ordinary glass.
  No invented vulnerability to a normal torch or iron sword. No claim that killing
  the single original Walker ends every danger in the forest.
HOOK DIRECTION: A familiar black cloak almost defeats the gate check; the
immediate danger resolves with Pell's intervention, making later checks matter.
STRUCTURE HINT: Chronological immersive survival story with a documentary spine,
seven movements and one sustained ranging, not an office/rank checklist.

## Identity and canon contract

Edren, Pell, Hobb, Torr, Orren, Lysa, the injured man, the maester and the patrol
events are original fictional people and events in a television-inspired Wall
setting. Hobb here is this original recruit, not a depiction or biography of the
canon cook with a similar ordinary name. Lysa is not Lysa Arryn. No actor likeness
or renamed canonical protagonist. All depicted human cast are adults. This is
not presented as an extra official episode, a date inserted into the TV chronology
or a claim about the first canonical discovery of the dead. The Lord Commander
remains an unnamed office; Edren never automatically becomes Lord Commander.

The temporary guarded shelter is an authored local decision, not a claim that
every era of the Watch welcomes the Free Folk. Volunteer recruits may leave
before swearing; Edren's actual oath closes that choice. Winter supply and
medical work are scene fiction, not sourced historical survival instructions.

## Angle search and opening selection

Default: wearing black means fighting wildlings from a giant wall. Hidden cost:
lifelong commitment and daily work in a hostile climate. Untold experience:
stewards/builders keep a ranger alive. Mechanism: a defensible gate also needs
someone to recognise the living. Selected blend pays a concrete new experience
instead of stretching a list of familiar characters into a supposed rank ladder.

Selected opening: "You nearly let a dead man through the gate."
Alternatives considered: a glove freezing during the lift (strong discomfort,
less supernatural identity); receiving used boots at recruitment (clear daily
life but too similar to workplace handoff openings). The selected encounter
resolves before context. No present-day threat is parked for a ten-minute rewind.

The actual Saiyan, Jabba, Viltrumite and Space Marine transcripts were fetched
in this session. Saiyan's measurement shows hierarchy immediately; Jabba gives
an immediately visible reversal; Space Marine turns an obstacle into an achieved
task. This script uses a mistaken gate identification and a completed local
defence, then continues forward. It does not reproduce a reference's wording,
scene sequence, narrator fragments or CTA. Its fear-led opening also contrasts
with the new Marine script's bucket comedy.
