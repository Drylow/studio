# One Piece Marine career — research and editorial review

Owner authorised production on 21 October 2026, before the Wall episode. This
review covers the authored narration, not generated images or a finished film.

TOPIC: Your Life as Every Marine Rank in One Piece.
ANGLE: A recruit who initially needs help to protect a ship gains enough command
to send help to neglected ports. Fighting ability, relationships and practical
authority change together; the viewer experiences each formal rank through events.
PROOF BANK:
- The eighteen formal ranks, in ascending order: seaman recruit, seaman apprentice,
  seaman first class, petty officer, chief petty officer, master chief petty
  officer, warrant officer, ensign, lieutenant junior grade, lieutenant,
  lieutenant commander, commander, captain, commodore, rear admiral, vice admiral,
  admiral, fleet admiral. [Marine Ranks](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Marine_Ranks),
  secondary reference citing the manga's SBS volumes 8, 24 and 29. Search-indexed
  text was accessible; direct page fetch was restricted. Chore duty is explicitly
  outside the formal eighteen; no fabricated corporal/general rank is added.
- The same rank page distinguishes the top Marine office from the World
  Government above it and identifies three admiral positions. It also supports
  the ordinary white shirt, blue neckerchief and dark trousers. No invented
  pay scale, insignia table, mandatory promotion timetable or qualification test.
- Official [Koby profile](https://one-piece.com/character/Coby/index.html): enlistment,
  training under Garp, Soru and Observation Haki development; exceptional growth
  is possible, not a guaranteed effect of a rank. Corin is not Koby's biography.
- Official [Garp profile](https://one-piece.com/character/Monkey_D_Garp/index.html):
  a vice admiral can be an exceptional veteran; rank is not a numeric power score.
- Official [Sakazuki profile](https://one-piece.com/character/Sakazuki/index.html):
  admiral and fleet admiral are separate offices. His appointment is not being
  replaced in an allegedly canonical event: Corin's entire career is imagined.
- [Buster Call](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Buster_Call) and its manga chapter
  409 reference distinguish the golden activation snail from an ordinary
  communication receiver. No lower rank is casually given an independent right
  to initiate it; the episode depicts this equipment only at admiral stage.
HOOK DIRECTION: A bucket-carrying newcomer stops an attempted theft, but the
immediate reward is more cleaning. End with a new recruit receiving help.
STRUCTURE HINT: An immersive fictional career with a chronological documentary
spine. Seven causal movements contain the eighteen ranks, rather than eighteen

are original authored fiction within the setting. They are not official manga

Three world-specific experiences: a Log Pose and dangerous Grand Line weather;

depends on rank. Keep these readable in actual images, not generic modern navy
offices. Golden snail stays unused: no island destruction as a casual reward.
