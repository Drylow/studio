# Your Life as Every Rank in a Space Marine Chapter

## Description

Your first job is opening a gate for warriors too large to fit through the
window. Survive long enough and you'll be the one deciding who goes through it.
Follow a Scout through battles, power armour, squad command and the choices
waiting for a Chapter Master.

An original animated Warhammer 40,000 story following Neral of the fictional
Cobalt Wardens.
