## Identity and canon scope

Cobalt Wardens, Neral, Corven, Damos, Leth, Eda, Ora, Veyr and all operations are
original fiction; not a claim that this is an official named Chapter or campaign.
Chapter customs vary. Neral's route is a representative command career, never a
promise that all Chapters use identical rules or every surviving Marine is
automatically promoted. Veteran is experience/status, not compulsory office.
Fire support/close support/battle line are duties. Specialists and Dreadnought
interment are explicitly outside the promotion ladder. Scout cast is drawn as
adult; childhood selection/implants are past, not depicted or graphically retold.
Chapter Master succession is a decision by this invented Chapter, not a stated
universal constitutional rule. No invented combat statistics or upgrade odds.
