## Writing brief

TOPIC: Living through Kratos' transformation from Spartan commander to the God of War.

ANGLE: Hidden cost, then responsibility. Each increase in Kratos' power creates a problem he cannot solve by winning another fight; fatherhood eventually changes how he uses that power.

PROOF BANK:

- Ares saves Kratos through a binding oath and later tricks him into killing his family. [Santa Monica Studio, Ascension single-player introduction](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/01/19/god-of-war-ascension-single-player-demo-in-february-collectors-edition-revealed/).
- Kratos serves Olympus for ten years, receives the mission to kill Ares, and becomes the new God of War. [PlayStation, God of War Collection announcement](https://blog.playstation.com/2009/08/31/god-of-war-collection-blu-ray-disc-compilation-available-this-holiday-season/).
- The ashes, continuing nightmares, Zeus' betrayal, Athena's death, and the destruction of the Greek order are established events. [PlayStation Germany, Kratos' development](https://blog.de.playstation.com/?p=158063).
- Faye's death starts a father-son journey to scatter her ashes. [Bear McCreary, The Themes of God of War](https://blog.playstation.com/2018/05/08/the-themes-of-god-of-war/).
- Kratos retrieves the Blades of Chaos to save Atreus; concealing the boy's godhood contributes to his illness. [Santa Monica Studio, official 2018 story recap](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/08/15/story-details-you-need-to-know-before-playing-god-of-war-ragnarok/).
- Killing Baldur saves Freya but causes her to seek revenge. [Santa Monica Studio, animated family portraits](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/10/21/god-of-war-ragnarok-animated-family-portraits-highlight-5-key-relationships/).
- Fimbulwinter and Atreus' desire to understand Loki create new conflict between father and son. [Santa Monica Studio, first look at Ragnarok](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/09/09/first-look-at-god-of-war-ragnarok/).
- Valhalla returns Kratos to the throne motif and forces him to face the man he was. [Santa Monica Studio, Past Lives, New Story](https://sms.playstation.com/stories/shaping-valhallas-narrative-with-kratos-past/media/1120).

HOOK DIRECTION: Begin with the bargain on the losing battlefield. Promise the life of a god while planting the question of what happens to the family Kratos expects to return to.

STRUCTURE HINT: Immersive second-person biography with a documentary spine, organized around changes in obligations rather than invented canonical ranks. Final payoff is Kratos' confrontation with his former self.

## Canon and dramatization

This is an original, condensed POV adaptation, not a complete walkthrough. It omits side adventures and does not claim to cover every game. The opening begins with adult Kratos; his childhood is outside the scope. Ragnarok is a short bridge into Valhalla rather than a detailed recap of its final battle.

Established events, relationships, and named objects come from the checks above. The viewer's internal reasoning, imagined battlefield sensations, hypothetical camp routines, and dry commentary are editorial dramatization. They are not new canon. There are no invented game quotations, combat statistics, exact ages, or fictional promotion ranks. The single elapsed-time claim, ten years of service to Olympus, is sourced in the Collection announcement.
