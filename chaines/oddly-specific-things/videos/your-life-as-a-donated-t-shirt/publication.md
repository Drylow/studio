## Description

A shirt leaves a South Philadelphia donation door as charity, but its price keeps shrinking every time a new hand touches it.

This follows one donated T-shirt from its first owner to the thrift-store rack, the outlet, the salvage bale, a New Jersey grader, Port Newark, Tema, and Kantamanto in Accra. Along the way, it goes from a $4.99 tag to a 1,000-pound cube, then to a sealed bale where the next buyer carries the risk.

More oddly specific lives here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlySpecificThingsYT?sub_confirmation=1

This is a second-person look at clothing donation, thrift stores, textile recycling, used clothing exports, and the hidden market behind one old Turkey Trot shirt.
