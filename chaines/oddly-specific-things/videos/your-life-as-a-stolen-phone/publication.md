## Description

A phone leaves Upper Street in one hand, goes quiet in foil, and becomes stock before the owner even finishes reporting it.

This follows a stolen phone from London to Heathrow, Hong Kong, and the resale-and-parts chain around Shenzhen. Along the way, it passes through riders, handlers, exporters, wholesalers, fake-message crews, and repair benches, with each hand changing what the phone is worth.

More oddly specific object stories here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlySpecificThingsYT?sub_confirmation=1

The numbers come from documented UK theft figures, Metropolitan Police reporting, Operation Echosteep details, and reported phone markets tied to Hong Kong and China.
