## Description

The crash is over in seconds. The car's value keeps falling through an insurer, a salvage yard, an auction, and a ship.

You start as an $11,000 sedan on Little Patuxent Parkway in Columbia, Maryland. After an 82 percent repair estimate, Maryland's 75 percent total-loss threshold changes your path. The title moves through the insurer, a Laurel yard, Copart, the Baltimore port, and a dealer in Cotonou. The numbers move from $10,500 to about $2,800, then back toward $8,800 after roughly 5,800 sea miles.
