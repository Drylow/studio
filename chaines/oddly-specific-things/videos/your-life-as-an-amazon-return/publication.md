## Description

A $99.99 air fryer gets refunded in Brooklyn, then drops into the quiet machine that turns returns into liquidation.

You start on an Amazon counter inside Whole Foods in Gowanus, Brooklyn. From there, you move through the return cart, the truck, the return center, inspection, Amazon Liquidation Auctions, a pallet buyer, and the resale chain that carries you about 1,420 miles away to Houston.

This is the life of an Amazon return. The refund looks clean from the customer's side. Behind it are returnless refund rules, FBA Liquidations, Amazon Resale grading, pallet auctions, Good360 donations, recycling, freight costs, and the strange math that turns a working $99.99 appliance into a $40 parking-lot sale.
