## Description

Eight fake bags enter JFK. Seven keep moving, while one is valued like the real thing.

You begin in Shiling, Guangzhou, where a low-cost copy is finished in a workshop and moved toward Baiyun World Leather Market. From there, you cross about 8,000 miles through the international mail system, pass JFK, reach a Manhattan storage room, and sell on Canal Street for about $60. The final stop is Pittsburgh, where you become an ordinary work bag.
