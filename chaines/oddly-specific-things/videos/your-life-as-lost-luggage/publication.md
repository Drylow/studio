## Description

A torn paper tag turns one black suitcase from a San Diego connection into orphaned inventory heading for Alabama.

You start in Pittsburgh with a yellow ribbon, a wedding outfit, black heels, a charger, and a checked-bag tag routed through Atlanta. Then the connection gets tight, the tag tears, WorldTracer gets a weak description, and your owner waits at an empty carousel in San Diego with receipts in her hand.

If you like ordinary objects with paperwork problems: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlySpecificThingsYT?sub_confirmation=1

This follows lost luggage through the real chain: airport exception handling, the missing-bag file, the claim process, central baggage handling, bulk salvage, Unclaimed Baggage in Scottsboro, Alabama, and the quiet second life at the end.
