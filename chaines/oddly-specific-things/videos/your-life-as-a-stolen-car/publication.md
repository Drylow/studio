## Description

A locked SUV leaves a Brampton driveway with the real key still hanging inside the house.

You follow one stolen car through the export machine: relay theft, a quiet test lot in the Greater Toronto Area, fake cargo paperwork, a container headed through the Port of Montreal, Europe, Ghana, and a used car lot in Accra. You start as an $80,000 family SUV with a car seat, sunglasses, a library book, and a blue crayon under the seat. By the end, you're listed for around $55,000 more than 9,000 kilometres away.

If you like following ordinary objects into strange economies, more of them are here: https://www.youtube.com/@OddlySpecificThingsYT?sub_confirmation=1

This is a second-person POV story about what can happen after a stolen car leaves the driveway, based on the real route described in the script: Brampton, the GTA, Montreal, Europe, Ghana, and Accra.
