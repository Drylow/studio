## Description

A locked $80,000 SUV leaves a Brampton driveway while the real key is still hanging inside the house.

This follows the car through the export machine in order: relay theft, the quiet test lot, fake cargo paperwork, shipping containers, the Port of Montreal, overseas movement, and the final car lot in Accra where it is listed for around $55,000.
