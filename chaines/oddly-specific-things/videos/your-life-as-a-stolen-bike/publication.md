## Description

By sunrise, an $8,000 electric bike is 50 miles south, and its photo is already for sale in Jalisco.

Follow one stolen bike from a garage in San Francisco's Inner Sunset to an auto shop in San Jose, across about 1,700 miles, and into La Barca, Jalisco. Along the way, its value changes from about $8,000 to about $400, then to a listing for about $2,700. Its serial number stays with it the whole way.
