Salahdine Parnasse against Ilia Topuria. It is the matchup suddenly getting attention, and there is more behind the conversation than a random fan poster. Parnasse's coach has shared a revealing message, and RMC Sport reports that Parnasse reposted a TikTok about the fight. But the same message contains the detail that changes the whole story: nothing is signed. Here is what those signals actually tell us.

ActuMMA describes two Instagram stories from Stéphane Chaufourier, better known as Atch, Parnasse's coach and manager. The first is a short, cryptic message: “La Parnassa.” The screenshot names neither an opponent nor a date. On its own, that is a tease. It does not establish that Topuria has been approached, that negotiations are taking place, or that the UFC has chosen this matchup for a card.

The second story is much more useful. Atch reshared a message from MMA Gang saying that he and Parnasse would not turn down the matchup. Its wording is conditional: if the UFC wants Topuria against Salah, the answer would be a big yes. That is an expression of interest from Parnasse's side. It is not a statement that both fighters have agreed, and it is not an announcement from the promotion.

Then comes the line that should stay attached to every headline about this story. The reshared message says there is no signature and, above all, nothing official. Those are not caveats added by somebody outside the camp. They are in the very post generating the excitement. Removing that sentence turns a willingness to fight into a booking, which is a much stronger claim than the available evidence supports.

There is also the TikTok angle. In its report published on October fifth, RMC Sport says Parnasse reposted a TikTok announcing a future fight with Topuria. We have not independently verified the original repost, so that detail is attributed to RMC. Even if the repost is exactly as described, sharing somebody else's video still does not establish a signed agreement. It makes his interest more visible, without settling the booking question.

RMC also explains why this pairing has traction. Its report says Parnasse made a striking UFC debut in Paris on September fifth, stopping Dan Hooker in two minutes and thirty-five seconds. Afterward, he called for a top-five opponent and mentioned Max Holloway. In that context, a message welcoming Topuria fits the direction his camp is publicly discussing: a bigger test after an impressive first appearance.

There is a reason the proposed opponent matters. RMC lists Topuria as the number-one lightweight and Parnasse as number nine. Those rankings are reported context, not proof of the next fight. They explain why the rumor feels like an ambitious step for Parnasse. A high-profile opponent would test him against a very different level of expectation, while a social-media tease alone tells us little about whether that step will happen next.

The timing circulating with this story is January, with RMC mentioning rumors around UFC three thirty-six. That should remain labeled as rumor. The screenshots do not provide a signed date, a venue, or a confirmed place on that card. There is also no acceptance from Topuria established by these posts. The headline can ask whether the fight is possible; it cannot responsibly present the card and the opponent as settled.

The next meaningful update would be an explicit announcement from the UFC, or a clear confirmation of an agreement from the people involved. More fan edits and more reposts can increase attention, but they do not answer the missing questions. Has an offer been made? Have both sides accepted? What is the date? At the moment, the public message establishes enthusiasm from Parnasse's camp while leaving those booking details unresolved.

So the development is real, but narrower than some headlines suggest. Atch has amplified a message welcoming the matchup. RMC reports a TikTok repost from Parnasse. And the camp's own message says nothing is signed or official. That combination makes this a story to follow, rather than a fight to put on a confirmed schedule.
