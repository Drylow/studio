**Description :**

Monaco, 1215 to now. It opens on the principality today, rewinds to a bare rock above the sea, then rebuilds it event by event: 811 years in 3D, from a Genoese fortress to 38,857 people on about 2 km².

It starts on 10 June 1215, when Genoese led by Fulco del Cassello begin a fortress on the Rock. In 1297 François Grimaldi, reportedly disguised as a monk, takes it. Then come sieges, a murder in the palace, the plague of 1631, Spanish and then French protection, and the Revolution. In 1861 Monaco sells Menton and Roquebrune to France and keeps less than a tenth of its land, so it bets on a casino on the bare Spélugues plateau: Monte-Carlo is born. The 1887 earthquake sends people to sleep in tents on the Casino square, trams climb the hills, and the Oceanographic Museum rises on the cliff. The first Grand Prix runs in 1929, the war brings occupation and the bombing of Fort Antoine, and Rainier III marries Grace Kelly in 1956. Monaco then wins land from the sea at Larvotto, Fontvieille and Mareterra, builds upward with the Odeon Tower, and ends in 2026 with the Pope's visit.

📌 ABOUT THIS RECONSTRUCTION
This is an illustrative 3D reconstruction, not archival footage. Building footprints and heights come from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps, the relief from the Copernicus GLO-30 elevation model. Towers appear in their real completion years and the land won from the sea in its documented dates; the dates of ordinary buildings are estimated from the documented development of each quarter. Lost buildings and landmarks are simplified. Population figures come from Monaco's censuses (IMSEE); extents and populations are approximate.
