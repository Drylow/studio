# Tel Aviv-Yafo 1880-2026: fact-checked timeline for a 3D timelapse

Researched 23 Oct 2026. Every figure below comes from a page that was actually read; the URL is given next to it.
Wikipedia figures were cross-checked against official sources (Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality statistical yearbook, Israel CBS, UNESCO-related wording) where possible.
Conflicting sources are flagged with **CONFLICT** and a safe on-screen wording is suggested.

## 4. NOT VERIFIED / OPEN POINTS

- **Tel Aviv head-count in 1909 and 1910**: no source found; only "66 families" (1909) and "around sixty homes" (1910).
- **Jaffa in the 1880s**: only the 1887 figure of ~14,000 (Wikipedia timeline, citing LeVine). Ruth Kark's demographic tables were not accessible.
- **Herzliya Gymnasium opening year** of the 1909 building: no source gives a separate opening date. Demolition year conflicts (1959 vs 1962).
- **Number of Scuds that landed in the Tel Aviv area** in 1991: not found (only "about 42" in total, aimed mainly at Tel Aviv and Haifa). The Israeli MFA page (38 missiles, 19 attacks, seen only in a search snippet) could not be opened (HTTP 503), so it is not used.
- **2022 census figure for Tel Aviv-Yafo**: the CBS census dashboard was not read; the two municipal tables disagree (463,670 Israelis in "IV.2022" vs 474.5 thousand at Dec 2022).
- **1948 Tel Aviv population (248,500)**: one press source only (Ynet), boundaries not stated.
- **ToHA 2**: completion not confirmed; height varies by source (298-303 m).
- **Beyond Office Tower**: topping-out date inconsistent (2024 vs July 2025); it is in Givatayim, not Tel Aviv-Yafo.
- **Azrieli Spiral Tower completion year**: conflicting (2026/2027/2028).
- **UNESCO page** (whc.unesco.org/en/list/1096) returned HTTP 403, so the White City inscription is sourced from Wikipedia only (2003, 27th session). Building count conflicts (4,000+ vs 5,000+).
- **Savidor station opening** (1953 vs 1954) and the year it was renamed after Menachem Savidor: not resolved.
- **Exact date of the 1950 name change (19 August 1950)**: in the summary of the Tel Aviv article; the body text only says "August 1950".
- **Habima building opening**: Wikipedia says built in 1945 ("occupied before completion"); no exact opening date.
