**Titre :** EVOLUTION OF TEL AVIV, ISRAEL | 3D CITY TIMELAPSE (1880–NOW)

**Miniature :** `thumb_a.jpg` (gratte-ciel) ou `thumb_b.jpg` (mer + ville).

**Description :**

Watch Tel Aviv grow out of the sand dunes north of Jaffa, year by year, from 1880 to today, in one continuous 3D flight.

From the Ottoman port of Jaffa and the first houses of Neve Tzedek, to the 66 families who drew lots on the dunes in 1909, the White City of the 1930s, the Declaration of Independence on Rothschild Boulevard in 1948, and the towers of the 21st century.

Every building on screen is a real footprint from OpenStreetMap. Building dates are an illustrative reconstruction: towers use their real completion years, the rest are estimated from satellite data (GHSL, 1975–2020) and the documented founding dates of each neighbourhood. Extents and populations are approximate.

Population figures: Tel Aviv-Yafo municipality statistical yearbooks, British Mandate censuses, Israel Central Bureau of Statistics. Dates and captions checked against Wikipedia and the sources listed on each article.
