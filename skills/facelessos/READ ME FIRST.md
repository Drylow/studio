# FacelessOS v5

The biggest upgrade to the system yet. Built on 8,000+ handwritten scripts and years of testing.

If you ran v4, this is a drop-in update: same setup, more skill files, a smarter workflow.

---

## What's new in v5

v4 started at "write." v5 runs the whole pipeline: **Research to Brainstorm to Structure to Write to Greenlight to Package.** Five new skill files do the heavy lifting.

→ **Research and ideation.** Finds the video before you write it: the angle, the proof, and how to research a niche you know nothing about.
→ **Voice anchoring.** Writes in your real voice, not the generic "AI narrator" voice.
→ **Humanizer.** Catches the specific tells that make writing read as AI.
→ **Anti-AI-slop audit (new and improved).** Every script now audits itself, five checks deep, and loops until it passes clean. It also catches its own overused patterns before you ever see them.
→ **Packaging.** Say "package it" on a finished script and get the full upload kit: title options, description, tags, a pinned comment in your channel's voice, and two thumbnail prompts.

You still get everything from v4 (16 files) plus these 5 = 21 skill files total.

## How to update your Project (2 minutes)

1. Open your FacelessOS Claude Project (or make a new one).
2. Upload every `.md` file from the `skills/` folder into the Project's knowledge. If you had v4 files loaded, replace them with these.
3. Type **`quick start`** in a new chat. It walks you through setup and the first script.

That's it. No new tools to install, nothing to configure.

---

## One tip that changes everything: give it your voice

When you set up your Project, paste a voice sample so it writes in YOUR register, not a generic one:

→ **Have uploads?** Paste 60 to 90 seconds of your own voiceover transcript.
→ **Brand new, no uploads yet?** Paste transcripts from 2 or 3 channels you admire in your niche. It anchors to how the lane sounds and lets you keep your own content. It borrows the voice, never the hooks or the topics.

Skip this and it still writes well, just in a generic niche voice. Feed it a sample and it sounds like your channel.

## The slop check (no tools needed)

Type **"slop check"** on any draft and Claude runs the full anti-slop pass right inside your Project: the banned-phrase list, the "AI voice" shape flags, the robotic-rhythm hunt, and the mechanical sweep, then reports every flag it found. Works on scripts, descriptions, pinned comments, anything. The greenlight audit also runs this automatically on every script, so you never have to remember it.

There's also a `trailer-voice-scan.py` file in this folder. **You do not need to run it.** It's the same check in script form, a bonus for the technical crowd who want to batch-scan files in a terminal. If that's not you, ignore the file completely. Nothing is lost.

---

## What to expect from a first script

Run the full workflow and you'll get a research brief, the script, and a **greenlight verdict block** at the end (the audit's report card). A fresh script usually lands on "FIX-THEN-PASS" with a short list of facts to verify before you record. That is correct and it is the point: the system brackets anything it cannot confirm rather than making a number up, so you never publish a fabricated stat under your own channel's name. Verify the bracketed items, do a quick read-through in your own voice, and you're ready to record.
