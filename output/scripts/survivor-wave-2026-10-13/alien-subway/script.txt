You reach the subway stairs as a white beam sweeps across the street behind you, lifting parked cars and tearing signs off storefronts.

You squeeze past the fallen map board with your gray bag in one hand. Below, the platform looks empty. But a metallic tap travels along the track toward the stairs. You stop before touching the railing. The next tap is closer, while the beam above you reaches the entrance windows.

You stop halfway down. Above you, the beam cuts across the ticket hall windows and turns the dust bright. Below you, the rail clicks again.

A woman whispers from the bottom of the stairs, "Don't step on the metal edge."

You look down and see her crouched beside a broken ticket gate. She wears a cleaner's vest with Mina stitched on it. One hand is held out low, warning you away from the trim strip beside your shoe.

Mina leads you down beside the tiled wall. An adult commuter named Ash is hiding behind the fare machines with a laptop bag. He asks whether the aliens have followed you from the street. You tell him the beam is crossing the entrance, and he follows Mina toward the lower platform.

A stranded train sits below with its carriage doors open and its lights off. From the tunnel beyond it, something knocks against metal. Mina leads you behind a concrete pillar as another knock answers closer to the train.

From the dark tunnel, a shape unfolds over the left rail. It has a narrow charcoal head like wet stone, with two flat feeler plates hanging from its jaw. Four long limbs grip the rail and the sleepers beside it. Its glossy plated back stays low. It does not look at the platform. It presses one feeler plate to the rail and waits.

The creature turns its head a little. Mina's mouth comes close to your ear. "We call it Clatter. It follows the rails."

Clatter taps the metal with one front limb. The sound runs down the tunnel and comes back softer. When nobody moves, it crawls past the train and disappears into the other tunnel.

On day two, Mina tests the platform with a mop handle. She taps tile, wall, and the concrete base of the pillar. Clatter does not come. Then she lowers the wooden end against the rail, barely touching it.

A knock answers from the far tunnel.

Clatter reaches the spot where the mop touched the rail. It lowers both feeler plates against the steel and taps along it. Ash whispers that he never wants to ride a train again. The creature does not turn toward his voice. Mina pulls the mop back without touching metal.

"Air doesn't matter much to it," Mina says later. "Rail does. Pipes maybe. Anything that carries a knock."

Mina unlocks the staff passage behind the fare machines. It reaches a maintenance room with a platform service gate and a concrete access tunnel heading north. Beyond it is a junction and the lower stairs of a high service walkway. The walkway has an exposed metal crossing before the concrete route climbs toward an exit near the bus depot.

On day four, you inspect the street entrance from behind the ticket machines. An upright alien marks a wall panel with a handheld device, then moves toward the broken map board. Mina calls it a Surveyor. Its pulse passes through the station wall while Clatter taps from the tunnel below. You retreat before either comes closer.

On day five, a crash rolls through the ceiling. Dust falls over the ticket gates. Ash grabs the nearest metal rail before Mina can stop him, and the vibration rings downward through the station.

You pull Ash behind the fare machines. Mina stays low beside the staff door. Clatter climbs halfway up from the platform, feeler plates scraping the metal trim. It does not cross far onto tile. It waits, taps twice, then retreats when nothing answers.

On day six, the street entrance becomes useless. A machine outside lowers a bright bar across the top steps, and the Surveyor fixes two blinking devices to the broken map board. You do not know what they do. Nobody offers to find out.

After the street entrance is occupied, Mina leads you through the staff passage into maintenance. Ash closes its ticket-hall door. You check the platform service gate while Mina listens at the concrete tunnel heading toward the northern junction.

The room has a platform service gate, a passage back toward the ticket hall and a concrete tunnel heading north. A ladder reaches a hatch jammed beneath street debris. Mina has already tried that hatch and shows you the bent handle. On the wall is a staff evacuation sheet. It marks the northern walkway and a warehouse shelter beyond the bus depot. Mina remembers doing a drill there in daylight. She keeps the sheet with the station map, but warns Ash that the printed route does not show what the aliens have blocked outside.

On day eight, you enter the stranded train through the service gate. You step clear of the metal edge and keep against the concrete platform wall. The driver's cab is locked, the power is off and Clatter occupies the tunnel ahead. You search the abandoned carriage bags while Mina watches the platform. Ash finds a coat and wants to check its pockets. Mina calls him back before he reaches the carriage coupling. Beyond it, the tunnel knocks again. He returns with the coat pressed against his chest. You take a sealed drink from a seat pocket and hand it to him. He asks whether the train could ever move again. Mina points to the dark cab and the rails disappearing beneath Clatter. Neither of you has a way to power it, and moving it would send noise through the entire tunnel. Ash puts the coat on and follows you back toward the service gate.

You take crackers and two sealed bottles. Ash finds a coat. Mina finds a folded station map in a driver's pouch, and that matters more than the food.

The station map shows a concrete access route from maintenance to the northern junction. A short staircase there reaches a dry landing beneath the high walkway. Beyond that landing, you must cross an exposed metal grid before reaching the concrete path to the surface hatch. From the junction, Mina can see Clatter tapping the grid's supports.

Mina tests that metal crossing with a loose bolt on day ten. Clatter responds from the track beneath it. Beside the junction wall is a short training rail resting on rubber blocks, disconnected from the running rails. A soft tap on that piece brings no response. Mina marks both results on the map.

Clatter reaches the connected grid and presses its feelers against the metal. Mina waits behind the concrete doorway until it returns toward the track. You ask whether the disconnected piece could draw it away. She says the soft test did nothing and you should not assume a louder one will work.

The next day, you find a cleaner's cart wedged between carriage seats. Its rubber wheels let you move supplies without dragging the bags. Mina checks one damaged wheel and tells Ash to guide that side while you push along the platform wall.

You load the cart with bags and push it along the platform wall. For a while, it works. Rubber wheels on tile make a soft bump. Then one wheel catches in a crack near the service gate. Ash leans in too hard. The cart twists away from him, rolls down the slight slope at the platform edge, and one front wheel drops onto the rail.

Ash reaches for the cart, but you pull him back. Its frame keeps rattling against the running rail. Clatter reaches that noise as Mina shuts the service gate behind you. The gate's metal clang turns it toward your new hiding place. It presses one feeler against the frame while all three of you move away from the door.

You leave the cart against the rail, with half the supplies still on it. For two days, Clatter revisits the service gate. When Mina checks its window, the creature is close enough to touch the lower frame. You can no longer use that entrance for quiet trips to the train.

On day thirteen, Clatter moves south toward a distant rail impact. Mina watches it pass the stranded train, then opens the gate. You recover the cart together, lifting its damaged wheel clear of the rail before rolling it back over tile. Ash carries the supplies it dropped. You close the service gate and leave the platform for good.

The next morning, you move north through the concrete access tunnel. The damaged cart carries most of the water, but one wheel catches at every doorway. Ash wants to fix it when you stop. Mina tells him to keep it on concrete and leave the metal fittings alone until they know what is nearby.

At the junction, you find the dry concrete room marked on the map. The low track tunnel lies east. The short western stairs lead to the sheltered landing before the exposed crossing. You can sleep on that landing without stepping onto the grid. Clatter patrols the supports below it.

On day sixteen, the Surveyor enters the ticket hall. Mina sees it marking doors and the platform service gate. The maintenance room will soon be approached from above as well as from the rails. She returns to the junction and tells Ash they cannot go back through it to his apartment.

Rain starts the following day. Water enters the lower passages until the tunnel east of the junction begins filling. You mark its level on the concrete wall. By morning, the water has passed the mark and reached the first step outside your room.

You move the supplies to the western stair landing. The surface hatch beyond the high crossing remains reachable from there, but Clatter rests beneath the grid and reacts whenever anything strikes it. Mina can reach the hatch only by creeping around a narrow maintenance ledge without carrying supplies. She checks it alone, then returns before darkness.

Ash watches Mina climb down from that ledge. He asks whether he could get back toward his apartment while the rain covers the street. He has family photographs there and papers he cannot replace. Mina points to the growing water below and the Surveyor's pulse coming from the ticket hall. She asks him to help carry the water instead.

On day twenty-one, Clatter comes through the flooded low tunnel with water running from its plated back. It reaches the grid supports and climbs toward the crossing. Mina taps the disconnected training rail with a wrapped handle while you watch from the stair doorway. At first, nothing happens. A harder strike makes Clatter turn toward the nearby clang, leaving the grid for the separate piece. When the sound stops, it returns.

Before dawn on day twenty-three, Ash begins repairing the cart beside the junction. You wake at its scraping wheel and ask him to stop. He says he is going back for the photographs, with or without the cart. Mina comes down the steps before he can move it toward the track.

Ash forces the bent wheel into its metal fork. The fork snaps and the broken fitting strikes the grid edge. From below, Clatter answers. Mina pulls Ash clear while you lift the nearest water bag. The cart stays tipped beside the connected metal.

He freezes, then keeps going. "My sister's pictures are up there. My papers. Everything."

The creature climbs onto the crossing and taps the broken frame. Ash looks at the wheel still in his hand. You ask him to carry a supply bag up to the landing. He stares at the cart until Mina repeats the request and puts the bag in his arms.

The dry landing is now your only refuge. Each supply trip from the junction must stay away from the crossing. Clatter keeps returning to the broken cart. You have to carry the water by hand while it is farther down the track, leaving the heaviest containers below until you can safely lift them.

Ash takes the heaviest bag on the next trip and says nothing about his apartment. On the steps, he asks whether you think the photographs are already gone. You tell him you do not know. Mina waits on the landing until he reaches her, then makes room for the bag beside her own supplies.

A torn cloth strip hangs from the cart's broken handle. Ash offers to retrieve it when Clatter leaves the crossing. Mina asks him to wait while she watches from the concrete doorway. This time he does. Together, you recover the loose handle and leave the rest of the cart where it fell.

The next days are spent moving the remaining supplies above the water. Ash returns for a container you could not lift, then passes it up before climbing himself. Your fingers ache by the time you reach the landing. Mina checks the map to find the shortest carrying route across the grid once Clatter can be pulled clear. A loose cable catches the last bag as Ash passes it upward. You hold the bag while Mina frees the cloth without cutting it. You cannot replace that container now. Ash reaches the landing and asks you to check its bottom before he sets it down. You find no leak and put it beside the others.

The separate training rail rests near the base of the western stairs, away from the running track. Mina has already seen that a nearby loud strike can make Clatter inspect it, although a soft tap sends no signal along the main rail. She wants to pull its attention there while you cross above. The creature will still be nearby. You cannot let a dropped bag call it back toward you.

On day twenty-eight, water covers the junction floor beneath your dry landing. Mina inspects the surface hatch using the narrow ledge. She returns and tells you a Surveyor is marking the depot fence outside. You stay below while it works. Even if Clatter moved away now, the hatch would open beside the other alien.

Mina checks again the next evening. The Surveyor has finished the fence and gone back toward the station entrance. The service yard leads to an access road and a warehouse shelter used during staff drills. She sketches that road from memory beside the map and tells you which building has the shelter door.

You prepare to leave at first light. Ash tests a cloth loop on the recovered cart handle without touching metal. It lets him pull the handle down against the separate training rail from the concrete steps. You place the bags in the order you will carry them across the grid. Mina watches the hatch until the Surveyor's pulse has faded beyond the depot wall.

On day thirty, Clatter returns to the connected crossing. Ash pulls the cloth loop from the steps. The recovered handle clangs against the separate training rail below. He lets it rise and strikes again as the creature turns away from the grid. Mina gives you the first water bag.

Clatter reaches the loud metal piece and lowers its feelers to it. Ash pulls again while Mina crosses the grid with the first bundle. You follow with the bag held clear of the floor. Ash releases the cloth only when you are over the connected metal, then carries the last container across after you.

You reach the concrete walkway as Clatter strikes the separate rail. Mina checks the surface hatch. The service yard is clear. She holds the opening while you pass the supplies through, then climbs out after Ash. You move beyond the depot wall before stopping.

At the warehouse shelter, Mina checks the low door beneath the awning. It opens into the room she remembers from the drills. Ash looks inside with her while you watch the road. There are mats, empty shelving and a second door to a courtyard. You move the supplies inside and close the street entrance. Ash puts his surviving photo sleeve on the shelf and places the water beside it. His other belongings are still across the occupied city. He sits down with you and Mina instead of asking to return.
