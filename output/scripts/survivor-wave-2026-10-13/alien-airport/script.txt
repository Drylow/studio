A silent craft settles across the runway as the departure board goes blank. You reach the terminal doors just as they lock. A pale scan passes over the glass. You move behind the check-in counters before it reaches you, but the baggage belt beyond them is still running toward the open hall. You need somewhere to hide that the airport will not expose for you.

The craft has landed between the runway and its far service road. It blocks the direct path to the small-aircraft hangars. Screens around the terminal flicker, and people keep pressing the locked front doors while the scan crosses them.

People run for the front doors and stop against the locked glass. A white scan crosses them from outside. You back toward baggage while its glow travels along the floor.

An adult baggage handler in a safety vest catches your sleeve before you step beside the moving belt. Her name is Ada. She pulls you behind a sorting chute and points toward the glass separating baggage from departures.

Open suitcases slide through the wall into baggage. Several phones glow in their pockets. Ada pulls you behind the metal sorting chute before the moving belt brings them closer.

An alien carrying a flat black plate crosses the public hall. It pauses at the ticket machines, raises the plate and leaves a white mark beside each glowing screen. Ada has seen it checking the entrances since the craft landed. A larger machine follows the same route later.

You watch the alien turn toward lit screens, door sensors and reflected light. It ignores someone crawling beneath a dark bench. A phone lights up near the coffee kiosk, and its head snaps toward that screen. You keep below the baggage window while Ada watches it pass.

By day two, you and Ada call the first alien the Surveyor. You watch it visit the departure board, door sensors and security arch. It ignores a person hidden beneath a dark bench. When a phone lights up near the coffee kiosk, it turns directly toward the screen. You switch your own phone off.

Someone answers your tapping from the arrivals-side service door. His name is Tomas, an adult pilot who was trying to reach the small-aircraft hangars. The locked terminal doors trapped him on the other side of baggage. Ada knows a staff route around the public hall.

Ada knows a maintenance corridor behind the belts. It runs below the check-in wall and reaches lost luggage, where a staff passage connects to arrivals. That could get Tomas around the locked terminal doors.

The Surveyor walks from the craft to the jet bridge, then along the terminal windows. When a loose cleaning cart rolls through the hall with its amber light blinking, the alien turns toward it. Ada throws a dark jacket over the lamp. The Surveyor continues past without checking behind her chute.

On day five, you test Ada's pocket flashlight beyond the baggage curtain, then retreat behind the chute.

The Surveyor approaches the glass and faces the flashlight. Its black plate glows. You stay behind the chute until it moves away, leaving a white mark beside the pane.

On day six, you reach Tomas through that passage. He follows Ada back behind the sorting chute and shows her the hangar marked on his clipboard. A four-seat training aircraft was inside when the airport closed. He knows how to fly it, but has not seen whether it is still usable.

Tomas says he can get you out if you can reach the plane and inspect a clear runway. Ada looks through the baggage window toward the silent craft. She asks how he plans to cross that much exposed ground. He has no answer yet.

Ada leads you through conveyor service passages to a staff break room. You recover sealed drinks and snacks, then take an airport map from an emergency binder. You carry your gray bag between refuges and leave it beside you when you stop. Ada uses the map to mark entrances the Surveyor has visited.

Ada opens the airport map on the floor. You and Tomas crouch beside her. The terminal lies north of the runway, the maintenance hangar south. A service road passes beneath the jet bridge and around the fuel yard. Ada marks the protected entrances you can still use.

On day eight, a broader alien appears beside a low lifting machine. You call it the Loader. It directs the machine toward equipment already marked by the Surveyor. You watch it remove a stair unit from the apron and carry it back toward the craft.

The Loader begins with the movable stairs near a gate. Its machine grips them and drags them toward the craft. The Surveyor checks the emptied space afterward. Ada points out that both are working from the same marked route.

The Loader tears away a wall panel beside lost luggage. Ada recognizes the old maintenance passage exposed behind it. That gives you a protected way toward the control tower, where Tomas might see the hangars and the Surveyor's route from above.

Before dawn on day twelve, you reach the tower and crouch behind its console. Tomas points to a green-roofed hangar south of the runway. The fuel yard lies between it and the terminal service road. Ada traces a drainage passage that reaches the road behind the nearest jet bridge.

Several fuel carts are parked inside the fenced yard. Tomas cannot read their labels from the tower. You will have to inspect them from the ground before knowing whether any can supply the plane.

From above, you watch the Surveyor visit the jet bridge glass, tower base and runway lights. It crosses toward the hangar when the Loader moves equipment there. Tomas marks the quieter stretches on the airport map.

Two days later, Ada leads you and Tomas through the maintenance corridor and drainage passage. You wait behind the road barrier until the Surveyor turns toward the tower, then cross to the partly open hangar.

The four-seat plane is inside. Tomas checks it without starting the engine. There is too little fuel for the flight he wants, and the battery is weak. He finds a labeled spare battery in the maintenance cabinet but cannot move it and the fuel cans alone. He asks the others to help clear a working space beside the aircraft. You ask where he could land beyond the airport. Tomas marks a rural strip west of the city on the map. He flew there before the invasion and remembers two sheds beside its runway. Ada asks whether anyone is still operating it. He says he cannot know that from this hangar. You agree to inspect it from the air if the plane ever gets off the ground. For now, Ada helps you move the spare battery beside the aircraft. It is too heavy to carry with the food bundles, so you leave it where Tomas can reach it before you return toward the terminal.

Ada finds a portable work light on a wheeled stand, powered by its own battery. She switches it on inside the hangar, then stops when the beam hits the open doorway. You point toward an empty baggage tug across the apron. You want to test whether the light can draw the Surveyor there.

The hangar becomes your second refuge. Ada brings supplies through the drainage passage while Tomas inspects the plane and its spare battery. You cover the work light with an old maintenance cloth. From the doorway, you watch the Surveyor return to each white mark left on the apron.

On day eighteen, the Loader removes the nearest jet bridge in sections. The drainage entrance you used loses its cover. The Surveyor follows the machine and records each emptied connection.

By the next day, the Surveyor has changed its route. With the jet bridge glass gone, it continues farther south and pauses beside your drainage exit. Ada stops the next supply trip before you step onto that exposed road.

You try the fuel-yard route and retreat before reaching its fence. The Surveyor stands beside removed runway signs, facing the road you need. By the time it moves away, daylight covers the crossing.

The Loader begins pulling apart the baggage claim area. Belt covers disappear, exposing the sorting room through gaps in the walls. Ada wants to move the food to the hangar before those gaps reach the chute where you sleep. Tomas argues that carrying fuel matters more. You offer to take the first supply bundle while they watch the passage.

On day twenty-two, a machine pulls the nearest baggage belt backward and jams it through the wall. The Surveyor reaches the curtain while all three are beneath the sorting chute. You hold Ada's sleeve to stop her crawling toward the supplies before the scan passes.

A phone glows inside a suitcase on the jammed belt. The Surveyor turns toward it. You keep Ada behind the chute while the Loader's machine pulls that suitcase away.

Once the aliens leave, Ada says you cannot sleep there again. She points to daylight coming through the torn wall above the chute. You make the next supply trip without waiting for darkness.

You carry the remaining food to the hangar. Tomas takes the empty fuel cans while you help Ada move the last bundle through the drainage passage. That night, metal tears inside the terminal. Your former refuge is being dismantled.

The fuel yard is reachable only during gaps in the altered patrol. The cart cannot be driven to the plane without crossing exposed pavement, so you plan to carry fuel in cans. Tomas checks the containers. Ada watches the road. You take the covered work light toward the hangar entrance.

First, you test the portable work light just outside the hangar, aiming its beam at an empty baggage tug across the apron. Ada stays by the door while you switch it on and retreat.

The Surveyor appears beyond the torn jet bridge. It walks toward the lit tug and records the bright patch on its side, leaving another white mark on the ground.

The following evening, it revisits that mark without you using the light. Your test worked, but added another patrol stop. Ada marks it beside the service road on the map.

During the first fuel trip, Ada and Tomas reach the yard while you wait near the work light. The Loader is removing runway signs farther along the service road. The Surveyor has disappeared behind the torn jet bridge, so none of you knows exactly when it will return.

A section of terminal glass collapses and reflects the emergency lights across the apron. The Surveyor turns toward it, crossing between Ada and the hangar. You switch on the work light and directs it toward the empty baggage tug.

The Surveyor hesitates between the terminal reflection and the work light, then turns toward the steadier beam. Ada signals Tomas to move while its panel faces the empty tug.

Ada and Tomas reach the hangar carrying a filled can together. You switch the work light off. Tomas checks the fuel recovered and says they still need another trip. Ada points toward the new mark beside the baggage tug. The Surveyor now has a reason to revisit their diversion site.

The following morning, you inspect the terminal from the tower. The roof above the old sorting chute has been removed. Ada looks at your face when you come back and understands before you speak. She had worked beneath that roof for years. After a long silence, she asks Tomas what they still need to carry.

Your next planned crossing fails before it begins. The Surveyor revisits the marked tug and blocks the fuel-yard approach. You shut the light off without using it. Ada proposes moving the same work-light stand farther across the apron so the known mark becomes only the first stop.

You move the covered stand behind a row of removed signs, leaving a cord attached to its loose cloth. Ada pulls the covering clear from behind the road barrier. The Surveyor reaches the familiar tug, sees the brighter stand beyond it and continues away from the fuel yard. You and Tomas use that gap to bring back the remaining fuel. As you reach the hangar, the Loader catches its moving door and bends the outer rail. Ada gets inside before the machine pulls the rail loose.

You transfer the recovered fuel while Ada watches the road from behind stacked tires. The hangar door no longer closes. Tomas replaces the weak aircraft battery with the spare he found during the first inspection. You put the supplies beside the rear passenger seats, keeping the pilot's controls clear.

On day twenty-eight, Tomas checks the engine for the first time. It starts, but the Surveyor turns toward the hangar as the propeller moves. He shuts it down before the alien reaches the open apron. Starting the plane will expose you. You need the whole takeoff path clear before trying again.

Tomas explains that the light will only buy them an initial gap. Once the plane moves into view, the Surveyor can respond. Ada asks him to point out the full route to the runway, including the seam where a heavy cart has damaged the pavement. They walk that short section together while you watch from the door.

The next day, the Loader removes runway lights at the far end. The strip Tomas needs becomes darker, but the lifting machine now works nearer its centerline. You and Ada watch until it carries another load back to the craft.

You and Ada inspect the runway from the tower one last time. The Loader carries removed lights back to the craft, returns by the service road and pauses while the Surveyor checks the empty sockets. Between those trips, the clear strip Tomas needs is open. Ada marks the remaining obstacle positions on his airport map.

Before dawn on day thirty, you uncover the work light at its new position beyond the tug. The Surveyor crosses toward that light while the Loader is carrying equipment back to the craft. Ada watches both from the hangar doorway. She signals you to return as soon as the service road clears.

You run back with your blue-gray scarf still around your neck. Ada holds the bent rail clear while you duck into the hangar. Tomas is already seated at the controls. You check that the bags are secured beside the rear seats and climb in after Ada.

The propeller catches. Its motion makes the Surveyor turn from the work light. Tomas rolls the aircraft out before the Loader returns to the service road. Ada keeps watching the torn terminal while you tell her when you have cleared the bent hangar rail.

The Surveyor runs toward you, but starts from the far side of the apron. Tomas follows the inspected strip between the empty light sockets and the craft. The Loader turns its machine toward the centerline from the other end. Ada sees its lifting arm move across the painted markings behind you.

The runway fills the window as the plane passes its pavement seam. Ada grips the seat. You feel the wheels leave the ground before the airport begins dropping below you.

When you rise above the runway, the craft is still grounded. The Surveyor reaches the plane's abandoned hangar route. The work light remains across the apron where you left it. Nobody can tell whether the aliens will move next, so Tomas keeps going toward the rural airstrip marked on his map.

You leave the airport behind, crossing fields while Tomas watches for the narrow runway. He used that strip before the invasion and described it while planning your flight. Ada points out the line of trees beside it before you can see the pavement.

The rural strip appears beside two dark sheds and a line of trees. Tomas brings the plane down hard enough to make Ada curse. Its wheels hold, and he rolls to a stop near the weeds at the far end.

Tomas shuts the engine down. You and Ada climb out while he stays in the seat long enough to catch his breath. You inspect the two dark sheds beside the strip before moving the supplies inside. You set your bag by the wall. Ada opens a drink and hands it to Tomas, who is still gripping his flight map. The airstrip is quiet. All three have made it out of the airport.
