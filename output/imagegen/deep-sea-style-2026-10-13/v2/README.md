## Actual image review

Codex inspected the rendered replacement at its native 1672 x 941 dimensions. The head is circular, both small eyes are visible, exposed hands/feet/face are white, and the cap, two-panel yellow vest, thin black limbs and readable foreground proportions match the approved drawing direction substantially better. The cabin, rail and deck hatch remain recognizable in the bow-to-stern view. One purple tentacle appears outside the rail.

Minor variation: the worried mouth is open with a small coloured interior rather than a straight neutral line. Mild environment gradients remain. This is a recorded visual review, not a claim of perfect identity or a substitute for the user's approval. The prototype's human review gate remains pending.

The paid receipt is retained exactly: identity was attachment 1, boat geometry attachment 2, drawing guide attachment 3. Its prose mistakenly called the drawing guide attachment 2. The saved prompts in `authored-scenes.json` now bind it correctly to attachment 3 for future execution. The receipt is historical evidence and is not rewritten to pretend the corrected prompt was already sent.

## Execution contract

`authored-scenes.json` contains the manually authored prompts and ordered reference lists for all three prototype scenes. The preset stores a reviewed canonical drawing reference; new projects freeze that reference and the character cast. Both existing Deep Sea projects have the same guide snapshot. Before a paid generation, TubeForge checks the guide's file hash and requires an identical, reviewed style reference in the shot. Missing character identity or a missing drawing guide is a validation failure, not an opportunity to silently switch references.

Background mode remains independently configurable. Free backgrounds still retain character and drawing consistency. This retake does not produce or validate the first episode, whose script remains a draft awaiting voice generation and timed scene planning.
