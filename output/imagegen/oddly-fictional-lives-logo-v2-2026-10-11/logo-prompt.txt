Create a distinctive, thoughtfully designed square YouTube avatar for Oddly Fictional Lives, an illustrated POV storytelling channel about living inside different fictional worlds, from mythology and fantasy to anime and space adventures.
Art direction: a polished illustrated mascot emblem with the richness and visual storytelling of a collectible comic cover, edited into a bold compact logo composition. Crisp black ink contours, rich matte colors, deliberate angular cel shadows, strong foreground silhouette, professional compositional hierarchy. The central white face must remain readable at thumbnail size. This is a completely new concept, with substantial costume and world design.
Main concept: THE MULTIVERSE STORYKEEPER. One adult cartoon explorer in a waist-up three-quarter pose at the center, wearing a deep violet hood pulled back around a bald, perfectly round pure-white head, a flowing ink-purple cloak, a charcoal adventurer tunic and one understated antique bronze shoulder clasp. The simple white face has two small black oval eyes, subtly half-lidded, and a tiny dry, knowing mouth. No realistic nose, ears, human skin, hair or beard. The mascot is adult and composed, with a slyly unimpressed personality rather than an angry esports monster or a cute baby. All visible hands are white simplified mitten shapes.
The explorer holds an open angular ivory storybook at chest level in both hands. From its open pages, THREE LARGE distinct graphic world fragments unfold upward and outward behind the explorer like dimensional paper silhouettes: on the left a vermilion anime-inspired wooden temple roof and low sunrise; on the right a cyan science-fiction doorway with one small distant moon and spacecraft silhouette; above and behind the head a cool blue-gray mountainous mythic realm with a single monumental stone doorway. Suggest each world with a handful of bold legible shapes and excellent drawing, not a collage of tiny details. Make the paper-world forms integrated into the book and cape composition. Their combined outline forms a dynamic compact diamond/fan, not a circle or Saturn ring. Use a small amount of cyan and warm amber light from the book to connect the elements; the face stays predominantly white. Clear depth, foreground character in front of the worlds.
Composition: center the powerful white face above the open book, with dark cloak framing it and the three colored worlds behind it. Face occupies roughly one third of image width; head, hands and book form a clean central focal cluster. Put the meaningful silhouette within a centered circular avatar safe zone with generous dark space at the corners. Near-black midnight background, subtle restrained light separation behind the emblem. No photographic galaxy, explosion noise, glitter, ornamental border, floating franchise logos, branded costumes, copied characters or random extra faces.
Create one finished 1:1 avatar artwork, not a presentation sheet, poster, mockup, banner or list of alternatives. No letters, initials, channel title, watermark or tagline. The emblem should convey one recognizable white cartoon person living many fictional lives, with a much more developed original visual identity.
